# BETAFPV HQ 65mm 2-Blade 1.5mm Shaft Propellers

## 产品介绍

This 65mm 2-blade propeller is ideal for toothpick builds, like HX100 FPV Quad. Lightweight and durable material, compatible with 1103 8000KV motor, suitable for 90mm-150mm DIY Micro Quadcopter Frame Kits.

For more options on HX100 FPV Quad, please check 48mm 4-Blade Propellers here.

### Bullet Points

- Produced with BC material with a strong design to be super durable.

- The diameter is 65mm, which fits for HX100 carbon fiber frame and toothpick frame.

- This 65mm 2-Blade propeller has a shaft hole of 1.5mm, which perfectly match the 110x motor, for example, the 1103 8000KV motor.

- Only weighs 1.6g/set. Included 1 set of 65mm 2-blade 1.5mm shaft propellers, 2CW and 2CCW.

- Product material: PC

- Weight(2CW+2CCW): 1.6g/set

- Color: Transparent Black

- Frame: HX100 Carbon Fiber Frame

- Motor: 1103 8000KV motors
