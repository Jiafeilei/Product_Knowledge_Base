# BETAFPV X-Knight 360

## 产品介绍

X-Knight 360 FPV Quadcopter - Special-designed invisible FPV drone that supports the Insta360 ONE R Camera, which will take FPV cinematic to a higher level.

To create a better FPV cinematic experience, BETAFPV & Insta360 released the second bundle product - X-Knight 360 FPV Quadcopter. It is a special-designed invisible FPV drone that supports Insta360 ONE R Camera. This quad is designed to capture airborne 360 views and hides the drone entirely from sight, which brings more possibility for aerial photography.
If you have the DJI FPV goggles, you can also choose the X-knight 360 HD Digital VTX. We also release the Insta360 Sticky Lens Guard to help pilots protect your Insta360 ONE R Camera. With this lens guard, pilots can enjoy their flight without fear of scratching. Meanwhile, this lens would bring you more choice on flight landing.

### Bullet Point

- X-knight360 is an invisible FPV drone with 5-inch props and a 4S battery, which is designed to carry an Insta360 One R. It is made to capture the aerial 360 views and hide the drone entirely from sight.

- Combined with the ONE R camera's latest 360-degree camera techniques, pilots can not only use the quad for FPV racing and freestyle tricks but also for cinematic and vlogging.

- X-Knight 360 adopts a flat design, combined with the customized 4S 1000mAh that is only 23mm in height, when paired with the Insta360 ONE R, you simply won’t see the X-Knight 360 in the sight, just like a flying camera.

- BETAFPV specially designed the BEC board to build a direct charge power system, not only convenient to power the Insta360 ONE R Camera during the flight, but also reduced the huge weight of the quad, pilots can feel a better FPV flying experience.

- Specially designed this innovative, versatile and functional X-knight 360 frame, is made of carbon fiber, and is durable and maintaining lightweight, which provides you a more durable and smoother flight experience.

- With the perfect match of lightweight 2004 3000KV motor and HQ 5025 3-Blade Props, pilots can get an excellent racing flight experience.

### Insta360 ONE R Camera

Insta360 ONE R brings innovative modular design, AI editing and the best quality footage yet, which makes the Insta360 a must-buy for action camera and 360 fans. It offers the resolution of a high-end action camera and the versatility and ability to reframe clips after shooting that you can only get from a 360-degree camera. It is a highly capable and versatile camera that’d be a great companion for vloggers and digital content creators.  What's more, Insta360 ONE R provides brand-new unobstructed and impossible angles, perfectly fit the needs of 360 FPV filming shooting. We also release the Insta360 Sticky Lens Guard to help pilots protect your Camera.

### Flight Controller

Equipped with the updated F722 2-6S 35A AIO FC(BLHeli_S), X-knight360 FPV Quadcopter totally supports a 4s battery for the stable-flying experience, providing a stable flight experience. Come with a BETAFPVF722 processor FC, BetaFlight OSD, and 35A BLHeli_s ESC, this board gives the brushless toothpick quad a more powerful flight performance that can push your FPV racer to the max.

### Motors & Props

2004 3000KV Brushless Motors is a lightweight 4S motor. It only weighs 16.4g / 1pc. Best matched with the HQ 5025 3-Blade Props, the motor is very efficient in use and has an excellent racing flight experience. Combined with the F405 Brushless Flight Controller & 35A BLHeli_32 ESC, it will provide you enough output power for stable flying.

### M02 5.8G VTX

M02 5.8G VTX board is a high-quality 5.8GHz VTX has 4 adjustable power including 25mW, 100mW, 200mW and 350mW. With 350mW output power, pilots can fly drones up to 600 meters away to explore more possibilities and fun.

- Only weight 1.7g.

- Smart Audio function is included

- Frequency: 5.8GHz 6 bands 40 channels

### Caddx ANT Camera

Comes with an ANT Nano FPV camera, a full-featured nano camera, which allows the drone to be flown with FPV goggles. It is ultralight only 2g, supports 1200TVL and Global WDR, 25°-45° adjustable degrees, perfect fit the X-Knight 360 FPV Quadcopter.

- Image Sensor: 1/3" CMOS Sensor

- Horizontal Resolution: 1200TVL

- FOV: 165 Degree

- TV System: NTSC or PAL

- Image: 16:9 or 4:3

- WDR: Super Global WDR

### Recommended Battery

4S 1000mAh Battery with XT60 connector is the best option to power the X-Knight 360 FPV Quadcopter. This battery is specially customized for X-Knight 360 FPV Quadcopter. With the flat design, the battery is only 23mm height, perfectly hides the battery from the camera's sight. With a 4S(14.8V) high voltage and 75C discharge rate, it gives the drone a stronger flight performance for short bursts.

### X-Knight 360 Carbon Frame Kit

X-Knight 360 Carbon Frame Kit is an innovative, versatile and functional design frame, which adopts carbon material becomes ultimate durability while maintaining minimal weight. With this special-designed frame, pilots can capture an aerial 360 video without any limitation - hide the drone entirely from sight.
