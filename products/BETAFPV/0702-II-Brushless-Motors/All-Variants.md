### The Second Generation, A Power Revolution

The upgraded 0702 II motors redefine performance with cutting-edge tile-shaped magnets, an optimized magnetic circuit design, and improved coil filling ratio. Compared to the original 0702, the 0702 II delivers a remarkable 14.8% boost in thrust, while the 0702SE II achieves gains of 13% (23000KV) and 12.7% (27000KV). Engineered to withstand the demands of intense flights, the new design virtually eliminates magnetic degradation. Whether you're aiming for Freestyle finesse or Racing supremacy, the BETAFPV 0702 II motors deliver unparalleled power and precision for your flight adventures.

### PEEK Screws

PEEK Screws is the ultimate solution for lightweight whoop, taking more grams off the build. PEEK screws only weigh 0.10 grams/12pcs, it can save up to 0.46 grams for a build that is using metal screws, while still being stronger and more durable than nylon screws. With more heat resistance, anti-friction, anti-shock, and anti-fatigue properties, the PEEK Screws are both reliable and durable. They are perfect for building 65mm/75mm size drones, including the lightweight Meteor series drone.

Note: Ensure proper tightening force when securing the screw to avoid damaging the nut.

### Propellers

For racing drones that require nimble and quick flight performance, the 1208 31mm 3-blade propeller is a suitable choice to pair with a 0702 II motor that has a value of either 27000KV or 30000KV. Alternatively, for freestyle drones that prioritize acrobatic maneuvers, the 1210 31mm 2-blade propeller is better suited for a 0702 motor with a value of 27000KV. The selection of appropriate propellers and motors is crucial to optimizing the flight performance of a racing drone, and can greatly enhance the overall racing experience for pilots.
