BETAFPV pioneers a breakthrough in efficiency with the industry's first 0.10mm stator core.

This ultra-thin design minimizes magnetic losses, dramatically improving energy conversion and reducing heat generation. Proven in testing, 0702 Brushless Motors (2026) deliver a significant efficiency gain of 0.1-0.2g/W compared to 0702 motors with a 0.15mm core.
