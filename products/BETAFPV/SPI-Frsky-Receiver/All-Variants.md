# BETAFPV SPI Frsky Receiver

## 产品介绍

### Specification

- Item: SPI Frsky Receiver

- Chip: CC2500

- Weight: 0.43g

- Connection Way: Pin-Connector

- Support Protocol: Frsky D8, Futaba

- Dimensions: 13.5mm*9mm

- Recommended FC: F4 1S AIO FC, Lite 1-2S Brushless FC

This SPI receiver comes with Plug & Play port, and is specially designed for the F4 1S AIO FC(No Rx) and lite 1-2S Brushless FC(NO Rx), no soldering work needed, super easy to install.

Notice: The default protocol is Frsky D8 (Frsky D16 protocol is not supported). If pilots need to switch the protocol to FUTABA, please kindly click the tutorial to check details.
