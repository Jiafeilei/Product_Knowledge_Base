### Advanced DCS Gimbal

Built on the advanced DCS sensor, the NexRadio DCS Gimbal marks a new generation of gimbal.

### Smart Sampling at up to 1 kHz

Under the V1 protocol, traditional digital gimbals output data at a fixed 500 Hz, while ELRS RF packet rates range from 50 Hz to 1 kHz. This mismatch can cause repeated gimbal data across RF packets. The NexRadio DCS Gimbal now meets the requirements of the V2 protocol, enabling smart sampling at up to 1 kHz. It automatically matches the ELRS RF packet rate for synchronized, responsive control.

### Ultra-high Precision

With 13-bit sampling depth and 8K resolution, the NexRadio DCS Gimbal provides the instantaneous, pure, and precise response demanded by discerning pilots.

Compared to Hall sensors, the inherent linearity of capacitive sensing minimizes the need for extensive post-processing, filtering, or calibration.

Precisely engineered geometry ensures balanced actuation forces across both axes for a consistent feel and smooth control.

Rather than applying generic factory profiles, every sensor undergoes individual dynamic calibration after full mechanical assembly. This eliminates stack-up tolerances and guarantees flawless electrical-to-mechanical centering for each unique unit.

### Anti-Interference

The capacitive sensor operates entirely independently of magnetic fields, rendering it immune to the electromagnetic interference that plagues Hall gimbals. Its end-to-end digital signal path further eliminates the noise and ripple inherent to conventional analog voltage outputs. Whether operating alongside high-power RF transmitters or within complex electromagnetic environments, the NexRadio DCS Gimbal maintains unwavering precision.

### Infinite Lifespan

Traditional potentiometers lose centering accuracy and linearity over time due to mechanical wear. The DCS sensor, however, captures position data without physical contact—detecting capacitance changes as the medium rotates. This contactless design ensures a consistently smooth, fluid feel with a virtually unlimited operational lifespan.

### The Ultimate Evolution of Gimbal Sensors

Moving beyond the mechanical wear of potentiometers and the magnetic interfere limitations of hall-effect sensors, the digital capacitive gimbal sets a new industry benchmark.
