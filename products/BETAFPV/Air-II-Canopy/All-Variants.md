# BETAFPV Air II Canopy

## 产品介绍

The next generation Air canopy is here. Engineered for peak performance, the Air II Canopy weighs an ultra-lightweight 0.5g — a 24% weight reduction that unlocks faster, more agile flight. Its crash-resistant design, featuring upgraded materials, strategic reinforcements and a redesigned curve, is built to take an impact and keep you flying. Easily adjust your camera from 15° to 45° to perfect your angle, enjoy universal compatibility with 9-10mm cams like the C02 and C03, and personalize your build with six vibrant colors. This is the ultimate, no-compromise canopy you've been waiting for.

### Bullet Points

- Ultra-Lightweight Design: Weighs only 0.5g, a 24% reduction from the first generation for improved agility.

- Enhanced Crash Resistance: Features an upgraded material composition and structural design, including a reinforced support brace, canopy cap vent and a new curve to enhance performance in crashes.

- Precise Angle Adjustment: Easily set your camera tilt from 15° to 45° in clear 10° increments for perfect framing.

- Wide Compatibility: Designed to be compatible with a wide range of 9-10mm lens diameter cameras, including C02, C03, etc.

- Multiple Color Options: Available in six different colors to match your style and build.

### Diagram

### Ultra-Lightweight 0.5g

Weighing only 0.5 grams, the Air II Canopy achieves a 24% weight reduction for significantly faster and more agile flight, making it the perfect companion for your lightweight whoops.

### Enhanced Crash Resistance

The Air II Canopy isn't just lighter—it's decisively stronger. Incorporating a more resistant material, strategic structural reinforcements, and a redesigned curve for superior crash resistance that protects your vital components.

The structure is significantly tougher and better equipped to handle impacts from all angles without failing. A strategically placed hole in the canopy cap absorbs and dissipates crash energy, reducing stress on the canopy and lessening force transferred to the frame and FC.

A thickened and strengthened front brace acts as a primary load bearer during head-on collisions, significantly reducing deformation and protecting your camera and FC from damage.

### Precise Angle Adjustment

Features innovatively upgraded installation holes that allow you to easily set your camera tilt in precise 10° increments, from 15° to 45°, to perfectly match any flying style.

### More Compatibility

The Air II Canopy's hollowed-out design reshapes the installation space to offer expansive compatibility, seamlessly accommodating an assortment of Micro and Nano Analog and HD Digital cameras like the C03, C02, ANT Lite, and any other camera featuring a 9-10mm lens diameter. With five color options, you have the freedom to personalize your build and make it uniquely yours.
