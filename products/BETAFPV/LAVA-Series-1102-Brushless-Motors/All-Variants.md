# BETAFPV LAVA Series 1102 Brushless Motors

## 产品介绍

The LAVA Series 1102 Brushless Motor, built for Pavo Femto, achieves nearly 130g peak thrust with advanced tile-shaped magnets, an optimized magnetic circuit, and improved coil filling ratio. Paired with the whoop's duct, Pavo Femto boasts a thrust-to-weight ratio exceeding 6.75:1. Designed for versatility, it features four M2 propeller mounting holes for compatibility with diverse propellers and whoops, making it ideal for various DIY projects. Prioritizing power, efficiency, and extended flight times, the LAVA 1102 ensures robust performance for every pilot.

### Bullet Points

- Designed for Pavo Femto: Tuned 14000KV motors paired with the whoop’s duct gain enable Pavo Femto to achieve an impressive 6.75:1 thrust-to-weight ratio.

- Outstanding Efficiency: Advanced tile-shaped magnets and optimized coil design ensure efficiency above 1.5g/W at peak 130g thrust.

- Lightweight Design: Weighs just 3.4g/3.6g, minimizing overall payload for better agility.

- DIY Ready: Includes 4 M2 mounting holes to fit a wide range of props and drone builds.
