# BETAFPV [BETA TEST] NexRadio 14 Radio Controller

## 产品介绍

The NexRadio 14 Radio Controller BETA TEST version has been out of stock, thanks for your participation and support!

Test it and share your feedback here. We’d love to hear your thoughts on grip comfort, ease of operation, DCS gimbal performance, and more. The design, core functions, and hardware are close to final, though minor issues may remain during beta testing. Your feedback will help us refine the experience and bring your ideas to life.

Meet the NexRadio 14—NexRadio’s first radio controller and the first to feature digital capacitive sensor gimbals. Built for precise control, reliable connectivity, and lasting comfort, it packs powerful performance into a compact, ergonomic design. Powered by an STM32H5 MCU and EdgeTX, it pairs NexRadio DCS gimbals with a dual-band ELRS Gemini-X TX module for smooth, responsive flying. Dual screens put essential information at a glance, while smart power management and modular construction support longer sessions and easy customization.

### Bullet Points

- Powerful EdgeTX Platform: STM32H5 MCU delivers high performance with the flexibility of EdgeTX.

- Compact, Ergonomic Design: Refined grips offer a comfortable, secure hold, while foldable dual-band antennas simplify storage.

- Modular Construction: Designed for easier expansion, upgrades, and customization.

-
NexRadio DCS Gimbals: Digital capacitive sensing with ultra-high precision, anti-interference, infinite lifespan and smart sampling provides smooth, accurate control. Now available!

- ELRS Gemini-X Connectivity: Dual LR1121 chips support dual 1W 900 MHz + 2.4 GHz GemX operation and ELRS packet rates up to 1,000 Hz.

- Dedicated ELRS Screen: Alongside the main EdgeTX display, a dedicated ELRS screen provides convenient access to settings and real-time Gemini-X TX combination status.

- Smart Power Management: Use while charging, with reverse-polarity, charge, and discharge protection.

- Efficient Cooling: A CNC-machined cooling system supports stable TX module operation at high output power.

- Refined Details: Clear digital audio, built-in 256 Mb SD NAND storage, and a tactile power button enhance the user experience.

### Specifications

- Model: N14

- Operating system: EdgeTX

- Operating voltage: 7.4 V DC (6.6–8.4 V)

- Operating current: 0.6–1.0 A (from minimum to maximum RF output power)

- Built-in RF: NexRadio TX Module

- RF Firmware: ExpressLRS

- Channels: 14

- Antennas: 2 foldable dual-band antennas

- Batteries: 2 × 21700 lithium-ion cells (not included)

- Charging: USB-C charging supported

- Charging power: 15 W

- Main screen (EdgeTX): 1.3-inch OLED

- Secondary screen (ELRS): 0.77-inch OLED

- Gimbals: NexRadio Gimbal

- Gimbal stick-end thread: M4

- External RF module connector: Micro/Nano module (not included)

- Firmware update: Via USB

- Dimensions: 180 × 141 × 77 mm

- Weight: 350g (without batteries)
