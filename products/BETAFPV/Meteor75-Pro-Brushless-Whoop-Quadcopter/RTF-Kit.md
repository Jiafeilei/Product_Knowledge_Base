### Meteor75 Pro VS Air75

The new Meteor75 Pro, equipped with the upgraded Matrix 1S Brushless Flight Controller, delivers enhanced performance with a G473 processor for 55% faster computing over the old version, a durable 1mm-thick board, integrated 5.8GHz 400mW VTX, and motor plugs for easier installation. Built for durability and beginner-friendliness, it requires no welding, making it an ideal choice for those prioritizing resilience and simplicity. In contrast, the Air75 Brushless Whoop Quadcopter is lighter, with a 75mm wheelbase, catering to advanced pilots seeking agility and performance to push the limits of their FPV experience.

### Powerful 1S Drone Combo

Meteor75 Pro with 45mm 3-Blade props performs very precise, agile, and fantastic flying, which is the largest 1S power whoop drone nowadays. Fit out with 1102 21000KV motors and LAVA II 1S 580mAh Battery, Meteor75 Pro has an excellent thrust-to-weight ratio performance.

Note: As of July 2026, the motors have been updated from 1102 22000KV to 1102 21000KV (2026). Flight performance remains unchanged.

### Flight Controller

The Meteor75 Pro, now featuring the Matrix 1S Brushless Flight Controller, combines enhanced durability, processing power, and adaptability for a superior 1S whoop experience. Its 1mm-thick circuit board increases resilience during intense flying, while the G473 processor enhances response speed and control precision. Simplified setup is achieved through integrated motor plugs, and its 5.8GHz 400mW VTX delivers stable, high-quality video transmission. Perfect for both indoor and outdoor FPV racing or freestyle, this high-performance drone is built for versatility and reliability.

The following information on the F4 1S 5A Flight Controller has been retained for your reference:

The latest F4 1S 5A flight controller is applied to all BNF versions of Meteor75 Pro. It features Serial ELRS 2.4G instead of SPI ELRS 2.4G for ELRS version compared to the previous version. The Frsky version reserves two UART ports for the available external receiver so it can be used as PNP. The ESC on the new board is powered by BB51 hardware in place of BB21. Last but not least, we update the gyro to BOSH BMI270 for better performance and stability since the new F4 1S 5A FC.

Note: For Serial ELRS 2.4G, the Betaflight firmware 4.3.0 and up start to support BMI270 gyro. For SPI Frsky, DO NOT flash the other firmware, otherwise, the RX will be lost in control at a very close range. Please flash betaflight_4.2.11_BETAFPVF411RX firmware built by BETAFPV for SPI Frsky.

### C03 FPV Camera

C03 is a lightweight 1.45g and 1200TVL resolution camera that has been designed to provide superb performance for micro FPV quadcopters. It has a 2.1mm lens that gives a 160° field of view, operating in 4:3 image ratios, and features a 1/3'' CMOS sensor for clear and brilliant images.

### Endurance Boosting Battery

Meteor75 Pro applies the new LAVA II 1S 580mAh Battery, which is capable of delivering a discharge rate of 40C (continuous) and 80C (burst). With its larger capacity and stronger power, pilots can enjoy more flight time and a robust flight experience.

### Meteor75 Pro Frame

Meteor75 Pro frame is the largest 1S whoop frame with 45mm propellers currently and comes with more features. The frame features a roomier battery slot for more battery choices such as LAVA II 1S 580mAh Battery. The motor holder is using a triangle bilateral stretch structure, giving pilots a more durable and smoother flight experience. In addition, the Meteor75 Pro frame offers seven colors for options, which pilots should purchase separately.

From 1S to 2S, indoors to outdoors, whether agility, maneuverability, speed, or stability, Meteor series whoop drones meet the needs of a variety of flight environments and offer excellent experiences to pilots. The latest Meteor75 Pro with 45mm props, being the largest 1S whoop, is proud of its performance both indoors and outdoors.
