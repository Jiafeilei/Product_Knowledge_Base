# BETAFPV 1404 4500KV Brushless Motors

## 产品介绍

We are excited to introduce the 1404 4500KV brushless motor! It is designed for the Beta95X V3 Whoop Quadcopter and Beta95X V3 HD Digital VTX, which could offer pilots a more smooth and more powerful flight, and lead the Beta95X V3 series drones to the next level. In the meantime, this motor is also a great choice if you're building 2.5-4'' ultralight micro drones with Toothpick F405 AIO 20A FC V4.

### Bullet Points

- 1404 4500KV brushless motor is designed for the Beta95X V3 Whoop Quadcopter and Beta95X V3 HD Digital VTX, which could offer pilots a more smooth and more powerful flight and lead the Beta95X V3 series drones to the next level.

- Come with 4 specially designed motor clips in the package, pilots can use the clip to increase the height of the props, which could effectively reduce the shaking of drones.

- Perfectly match with Toothpick F405 AIO 20A FC V4, this motor will bring pilots a powerful flight experience.

- It is lightweight, durable and plenty of power! Combing with the perfect match of D63 3-blades props, pilots could get an amazing flight experience!

- The plug of the BETAFPV 1404 4500KV motor is a micro JST-1.25 3-pin connector, with 28AWG cables. Just plug and play, no soldering required.

### Recommended Parts

- Flight Controller: Toothpick F405 AIO 20A FC V4

- Compatible Propellers: Gemfan D63 3-Blades Props

- Battery: 450mAh 4S 75C Lipo Battery

- Frame: Beta95X V3 Frame Kit

### Specification

- Weight: 8.49g  (1 pcs)

- Shaft length: 3mm

- Shaft diameter: 2mm

- Motor Mount Holes: 4-M2EQS-φ9

- KV (rpm/V): 4500KV

- Input Voltage: 3-4S LIPO

- Cables: 40mm long, 28AWG cables
