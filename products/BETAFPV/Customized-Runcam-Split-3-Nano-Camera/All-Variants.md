# BETAFPV Customized Runcam Split 3 Nano Camera

## 产品介绍

The RunCam Split 3 Nano, Ultra-low weight 10g, gives you 1080p/60fps HD recording while also utilizing a sharp WDR FPV camera. Betafpv customized shorter RunCam lens length for Canopy, which can prevent lens damage during flight.

The Runcam Split 3 Nano Camera is used on HX115 HD Toothpick Drone.

### Features

- 1080P/60fps HD recording & WDR FPV camera

- Low latency TV-OUT (40MS)

- Power-off protection for video files

- Wide voltage (5-20V)

- FPV aspect ratio 16:9/4:3 switchable

- Stronger flexible ribbon cable

- Optimize TV-OUT interference to reduce ripple

Specifications of Camera

- Field of View(FOV): Recording FOV: 165°; FPV FOV: 165°@16:9, 130°@4:3

- Video Resolution: 1080@60fps / 1080@50fps / 1080@30fps / 720@60fps

- Video File Format: MOV

- Image Resolution: 2 MP

- TV Mode: NTSC (720*480) / PAL (720*576) Switchable

- Interface: JST 1.25mm / UART

- Max Micro SD Card Supported: 64G(need Class 6 or above, recommend Class 10 / UHS-I / UHS-II / UHS-III)

- Hole Distance of Installation: 25*25mm

- Dimensions: PCB 29*29mm / Lens Module 14*14mm

- Power Input: DC 5-20V(Non-direct power supply from battery. Powered direcly with battery will generate surges and burn the camera.)

- Working Current: 650mA @5V / 270mA @12V

- Weight: 10g

### Package

- 1 * Customized Runcam Split 3 Nano Camera

- 1 * Runcam OSD board and cable

- 1 * Set of M2 screws
