# BETAFPV HX115 T700 Carbon Fiber Frame

## 产品介绍

HX115 frame adopts the spraying process with an insulating effect on frame surface, effectively reducing the possibility of component damage caused by electric current.

### Bullet Point

-
Newly structure design, R0.5 chamfer, and 4mm thickness make HX115 T700 carbon unibody frame become ultimate durability and aesthetic while maintaining minimal weight

- Apply upgraded T700 carbon fiber material, stronger crashworthiness compared to the initial T300 frame.

- Package includes no-slip rubber pads, screws and battery cable tie for easier self-assembly

### Specification

- Material: Carbon Fiber

- Diagonal motor distance: 115mm

- Thickness: 4mm

- Motor mounting hole: M2*4

- FC mounting hole: 26.5mm x 26.5mm

- Weight: 9.4g
