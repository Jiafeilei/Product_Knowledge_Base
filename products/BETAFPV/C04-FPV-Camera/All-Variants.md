# BETAFPV C04 FPV Camera

## 产品介绍

Designed specifically for micro FPV quadcopters, the C04 FPV Camera packs premium imaging into a lightweight 2.74g body. It delivers a 1200TVL resolution and an expansive 160° field of view, driven by a 1/3″ CMOS sensor that produces vibrant, true‑to‑life colours. As a direct drop‑in replacement for the Aquila20 Analog, it serves as the perfect emergency backup to minimise downtime. Better still, it is compatible with virtually any whoop frames with 14mm camera mount, giving you a versatile upgrade or spare for multiple builds.

### Bullet Points

- Ultra‑Light & Compact – Weighs only 2.74g, minimising added weight on micro builds without compromising durability or performance.

- High‑Definition Imaging – 1200TVL resolution paired with a 1/3″ CMOS sensor delivers crisp, clear imagery.

- Wide 160° Field of View – Captures expansive, immersive scenes — perfect for close‑quarters racing and freestyle exploration.

- Direct Compatibility – Seamlessly replaces the Aquila20 Analog camera, and fits any whoop frame with a 14mm camera mount — making it a versatile spare for quick field repairs and reduced downtime.
