# BETAFPV Micro Canopy for HD Camera

## 产品介绍

This micro canopy is specially designed for Walksnail Avatar Nano camera, Runcam Nano Lite Camera, or most 14mm nano cameras, supporting an adjustable camera lens of 0-45°. It features high compatibility for both HD Digital VTX (P1 Air Unit) and Analog VTX (M03 Analog VTX).

### Bullet Points

- The canopy is designed with camera mounting holes for an adjustable camera lens of 0-45°. It is suitable for Walksnail Avatar Nano camera, Runcam Nano Lite Camera, or most 14mm nano cameras.

- Lightweight and two colors for options (White/Black/Blue). Using it with Meteor Series colorful frame will be more attractive. Recommend frames for Meteor75 Pro P1, Meteor65 Pro, Meteor75, and Meteor85.

-
Adopt with injection molding technique, this canopy is more durable, anti-ruin, and heat-resistant, providing good protection for the whoop drone.

- Unique design with three decorative parts like a knight, the drone flies in the air like a scientific spaceship cruising, making it very cool and special. Also, the decorative parts are detachable for better heat dissipation.

### Specification

- Item: Micro Canopy for HD Camera

- Material: PP

- Tilt: 0-45°

- Weight: 2.50g (without decorations), 3.28g (with decorations)

- Color: Black/Blue

- Adapted VTX: P1 Air Unit, 1S Walksnail Avatar Mini HD VTX, HDZero Whoop Lite Bundle, M03 25-350mW 5.8G VTX

- Adapted FPV Camera: P1 Camera, Walksnail Avatar Nano Camera, Runcam Nano Lite Camera, or most 14mm nano cameras

- Adapted Frame: Meteor75 Pro, Meteor65 Pro/Meteor75/Meteor85

### New Update, High Compatibility

It is the stock canopy for Meteor75 Brushless Whoop Quadcopter (1S HD Digital VTX) and is outstanding in more ways than one. Sporting a hollow-out design on the whole canopy, it allows more air to flow, greatly reducing the influence of heat on electronic parts. In addition, using it with Meteor75 Pro P1, Meteor65 Pro, Meteor75 (Analog), and Meteor85 is a good choice for those pilots who want to upgrade their drones to HD Digitial VTX.
