# BETAFPV Customized Naked Camera

## 产品介绍

We are sorry to announce that Customized Naked Camera is discontinued and will not be available for sales anymore. We recommend you buy its great alternative SMO 4K camera.

Do you really need a quad with the heavy ducts to carry on a GoPro for filming? No, what you need indeed is a super light BEC board for your GoPro, make it lighter from 120g to 29g, and easier to install. So we decrease the used GoPro HERO6 camera and assemble it one by one to make the Naked Camera. If you have a GoPro HERO6/7, you can also choose the BEC board and case to build one as our GoPro lite camera.

We bought the used GoPro HERO6 from the market, decrease and change it to this light version. Really sorry we could not provide enough GoPro Lite because of the limited quantity of used GoPro. Only 15pcs is available for retail now.

Note: The green LED on the BEC board of Naked camera may flash quickly, which may influence the normal indication of the red LED. But please don't worry, It will not affect any function of Naked Camera.

### Bullet Point

- GoPro Lite camera is specially designed for micro whoop drone like Beta85X and Beta95X which will be a perfect tool for FPV filming. Lightweight of 29g and easy to install on your drone.

- We decase the used GoPro HERO6 camera and assemble it one by one. The firmware is changed to 1.6 version and the default mode is in record 2.7K@60fps NTSC 4:3, NO image stabilization on.

- The BEC output voltage is 5V@2A, avoid the accident of power off which is caused by the insufficient power supply for the FC board. Also, stock buttons and LED are integrated.

- With the pluggable connector, which is easier to connect with your flight controller for power supply and recording.

- Packed with the GoPro Lite BEC Board, carbon fiber board and 3D printing parts, easier installation and better to protect your GoPro motherboard.

### Diagram

Under normal operation, plug the GH1.25 connector into the GoPro Lite camera firstly, then connect with the battery. The LED will flash once, then you should press the Power / Mode button once in a second to power on. The LED on the BEC board has the same status as the stock LED on the GoPro mainboard.

### WIFI Connection

The GoPro WIFI is on in default. But if you wanna set up the GoPro via your phone, you need the help of the LCD monitor for the first connection. First, download the GoPro App on your phone. Then connect the Touch LCD Display connector with the original GoPro monitor, and press the Power Button to turn on the GoPro. Next, you could connect your phone with the GoPro following the manual.

Note: Only need once for the connection process above. The GoPro could connect with your phone without the LCD display next.

Note: The connection is usually slow for the first time. The scan process takes about one minute.

### How to Setup Remote Record

Pilot could use the switch on the radio transmitter to start/stop record via Betaflight Pinio function. The configuration depends on which FC board and Pin IO used. For more details about the configuration manual, please check the BEC board for help.

### LED Status

Under normal operation, plug the GH1.25 connector into the GoPro Lite camera firstly, then connect with the battery. The LED on the BEC board has the same status as the stock LED on the GoPro mainboard. For more details about the LED status, please check the BEC board for help.
