### Bullet Points

- FPV Micro AIO Camera 5.8G 40CH 25mW Transmitter for whoop drone Blade Inductrix.

- Light weight only 3.6g, Small size with 47 * 14.8 * 12.5 mm.

- Supporting touch switch select channel Frequency group,Supporting NTSC/PAL switch, Support vertical inverted image switch.

- 8 LEDs display channel and 5 LEDS display frequency group information, 1 LED display PAL/NTSC and FLIP information.
