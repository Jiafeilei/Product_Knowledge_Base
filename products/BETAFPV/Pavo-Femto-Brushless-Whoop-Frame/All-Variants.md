# BETAFPV Pavo Femto Brushless Whoop Frame

## 产品介绍

The Pavo Femto Brushless Whoop Frame is designed for performance and durability, offering a 4% thrust increase in a compact structure. It features improved connections between the whoop duct and carbon fiber plate with added supports and limits for secure assembly. Optimized for the DJI O4 Air Unit, it includes a reinforced protective structure for the circuit board. Additionally, the O4 Air Unit Bracket is compatible with Pavo Femto, Pavo Pico and Pavo20, catering to various FPV builds.

### Bullet Points

- Enhanced Assembly: Improved connection between the whoop duct and carbon fiber plate with added supports for secure, hassle-free assembly.

- Boosted Thrust: Smaller duct design increases thrust by approximately 4%.

- Improved Durability: Strengthened protection for DJI O4 Air Unit's circuit board.

- Wide Compatibility: O4 Air Unit Bracket works seamlessly with Pavo Femto, Pavo Pico and Pavo20.

### 4% Thrust Boost

Inspired by rocket engine nozzle design, the Pavo Femto Frame optimizes whoop duct aerodynamics with precise height and thickness adjustments. Despite its smaller size, the frame achieves an approximate 4% thrust increase, providing enhanced power and stability. This allows pilots to execute advanced maneuvers like flips and turns with precision, ensuring smooth and reliable performance even in challenging conditions.

### Enhanced Assembly

Features an improved connection between the whoop duct and the carbon fiber plate with added supports for a secure fit, ensuring quick and hassle-free assembly. Say goodbye to loose nuts and tedious installations.

### More Protection

Maintains the modular design while upgrading the DJI O4 Air Unit Bracket with a reinforced protective structure for its circuit board, enhancing durability and reliability during use.

### O4 Air Unit Bracket

The O4 Air Unit Bracket includes the O4 Camera bracket and Shock-Absorbing Components for O4 Air Unit, ensuring stable performance for high-quality footage. Its mounting holes are fully compatible with Pavo Femto, Pavo Pico, and Pavo20, making it ideal for various builds.
