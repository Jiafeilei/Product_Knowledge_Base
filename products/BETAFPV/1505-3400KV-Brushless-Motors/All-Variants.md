# BETAFPV 1505 3400KV Brushless Motors

1505 3400KV Brushless motor is designed for Pavo30 3-inch pusher cinewhoop Drone . Combined with Gemfan D76 5-Blades propellers, it offers the drone a powerful flight and less noise. In the meantime, we highly recommend this motor for building a 3-4S quadcopter with F722 AIO 35A Brushless flight controller.

### Bullet Points

- 1505 3400KV brushless motor is designed for Pavo30 Whoop Drone, which could offer pilots a more smooth and more powerful flight and lead the Beta95X V3 series drones to the next level.

- Gemfan D76 5-Blades props will be the best match with 1505 3400KV motor, this high efficient power system lays the foundation for powerful flight and less noise.

- Perfectly match with F722 AIO 35A Brushless flight controller, this motor will bring pilots a powerful flight experience with 4S battery
