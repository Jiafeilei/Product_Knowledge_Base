# BETAFPV Beta65X HD Whoop Quadcopter (2S)

## 产品介绍

We are sorry to announce that Beta65X HD is discontinued and will not be available for sales anymore. We recommend you buy its great alternative Meteor65 HD.

Beta65X HD - The Lightest and Smallest Brushless Cinewhoop in the world!

This is a thrilling classical brushless cinewhoop-Beta65X HD! Classical size of 65mm and lightweight in 30g only. Adopted with the high-definition 1080P@60fps BETAFPV Nano HD Camera, lead you to a fairly clear image of every interesting moment with your family and your pet. Stay home and have fun with Beta65X HD. We also provide a Beta65X FPV version for your choice.

Videos of Beta65X HD

### Bullet Point

-
Beta65X HD is the lightest and smallest brushless cinewhoop at the world! Small size in 65mm and light weight of 30g, gives pilots a good balance of weight and nice punch on 2S battery.

- Installed with the lightest BETAFPV Nano HD camera. Ultra definition with 1080P@60fps resolution, super-light weight with 6.8g, complete functions with OSD, audio and record.

- Customized with the lightest 0802 14000KV motors, give you a extreme power no matter you fly indoor or outdoor.

- Soldered with the BT2.0 connector, which can provide a stable and powerful continuous current so that you can extend the fligth time to 3:00-4:00mins compared to the Beta65 pro 2.

- Used Meteor65 frame and adopted the stackable battery slot to cut down on the wind resistance.

- With the high performance STM32F411 processor FC boards and betaflight_matekf411rx_4.0.0 (Development Version).

### Nano HD Camera & VTX

BETAFPV Nano HD Camera -This is the Lightest HD camera at the current market! Ultra definition with 1080p resolution, super-light weight with 6.8g, complete functions with OSD, audio and record.  Also, it adopts a 1/2.7" CMOS sensor and a 1.8mm lens for excellent latency. Best choice for all whoop drones like Beta65X / Beta75X / Beta85X. Discover the beauty of the world and record the wonderful life!

The stock VTX on the 65X 2S drone is the M series 25mW VTX.

Note: the 25mW VTX is marked as M02 on the first batch. It will be changed to M01 V2 since the second batch). Compared with the original M01 VTX, the 5V pad change its position.

With the high definition of 1080p resolution, you will have a fairly clear image.

The camera tilt is 30° which is unswitchable right now.

### Flight Controller

This F4 AIO 2S FC is suitable for a lighter 1-2S whoop drone perfectly, like Beta65X，Beta65 Pro 2 or Beta75 Pro 2 etc. It integrated with a Betaflight F411 processor FC, Betaflight OSD, SPI Frsky receiver and 1-2S 5A ESC. So the 1-2S drone could save much weight for a faster fly experience.

### Meteor65 Frame

Meteor65 frame is the most popular and colorful 65mm frame in this winter.

We only need to add 0.3g 3D printed adapter and change it to a 2S 65mm frame. Still light enough and you could experience the aggressive power of 2S now.

Note: The STL file for this 2S battery adapter is public and you could print it by yourself.

### BT2.0 Connector

BETAFPV exclusive designed BT2.0 connector has broken through the bottle-neck of PH2.0 connector. BT2.0 connector has decreased internal resistance of connector effectively allowing more current to be transferred, which means it supports 9A continuous current (15A burst). This means stronger power and longer flight time on 1S brushless drones while PH2.0 just supports 4.5A. You can enjoy more flight time without disturb of low voltage warning due to reducing voltage sag. For more instruction, click here.
