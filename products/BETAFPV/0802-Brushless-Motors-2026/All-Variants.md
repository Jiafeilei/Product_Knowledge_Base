This ultra-thin design minimizes magnetic losses, dramatically improving energy conversion and reducing heat generation. Proven in testing, 0802 Brushless Motors (2026) deliver a significant efficiency gain of 0.1-0.2g/W compared to 0802 motors with a 0.15mm core.
