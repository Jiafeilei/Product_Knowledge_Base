# BETAFPV Gemfan 1207 3-Blade Propellers (1.0mm Shaft)

## 产品介绍

The Gemfan 1207 3-Blade Propeller (1.0mm Shaft) is a state-of-the-art micro prop designed for high-performance micro drones. Constructed from high-strength PC material, it features a 31mm diameter and a 0.7-inch pitch while weighing only 0.15g—making it the lightest 31mm propeller available. Its innovative design minimizes rotational inertia for superior flight performance, delivering faster response and precise control. Available in Transparent Gray and Transparent to match your style, these propellers are the perfect match for 0702 Brushless Motors (2026) on the Air65 II Champion and Racing editions.
