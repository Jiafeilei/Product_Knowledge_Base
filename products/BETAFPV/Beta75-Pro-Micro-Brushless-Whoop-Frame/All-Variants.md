# BETAFPV Beta75 Pro Micro Brushless Whoop Frame

## 产品介绍

From the picture below, you can see the difference of the Beta75pro frame of different versions.

Please note that the OG 75pro frame doesn't fit 0703 19000KV motors.

Here are the IMPROVEMENTS we've made.

### Recommended Parts

- Propellers: 40mm 4-blade props

- Motors: 0703 brushless motors

- Batteries: 550mAh 1S Lipo HV batteries

- Frame: Beta75 Pro frame

Really not recommend the 0703 motors with a 65mm frame ( like Beta65 Pro frame or BWhoop B06 etc). Why? The combo of 0703 motors and 31mm props (for 65mm frame) has a really low efficiency. Really not power enough. For the 65mm 1S brushless drone, recommend the 0603 motors (comming soon).

Here is the testing result of different motors and frames combo.

-  Throttle value means the position when the drone hovers in the air.

- Flying time is the time from OSD voltage value 4.3V->2.8V.

- All tested with 4-blades props. 31mm props or 40mm props.

- All throttle values are tested on full charged 550mAh batteries.

- The KV value of 0703/0603 motors are both 15000KV.
