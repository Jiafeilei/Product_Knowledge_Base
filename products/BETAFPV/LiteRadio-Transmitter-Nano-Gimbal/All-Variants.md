# BETAFPV LiteRadio Transmitter Nano Gimbal

## 产品介绍

This is the original joystick gimbal for LiteRadio 3 and LiteRadio 2 SE. It functions well in its durability, bringing a good flight experience to pilots. Besides, the gimbal with wires makes it very easy to replace. For customers who want to replace or upgrade the gimbal for LiteRadio 3 or LiteRadio 2 SE, this gimbal has a good performance and reasonable price.

### Bullet Points

- Made of high-quality plastic and metal. With ergonomic stick ends, the stick is detachable, anti-slip, and adjustable in height.

- Potentiometer gimbal is long longevity up to 120k times rotations. The gimbal with wire is very easy to replace.

- The potentiometer is positioning accuracy and fast response sensitivity, bringing a good experience to your flight.

- Hall gimbal features four precision bearings, adjustable spring tension, and ergonomic stick ends for an unparalleled feel.

- The throttle/yaw stick is used for acceleration and can not be back to the middle automatically. While the pitch/roll stick controls the direction and can be back to the middle.

### Hall Gimbal

The nano gimbal with hall sensor is released, featuring four precision bearings, adjustable spring tension, and ergonomic stick ends for an unparalleled feel. With an adjustable stick end, it perfectly fits thumbs and improves the stick feel for the control. Hall Effect provides contactless sensing for not only accurate control inputs and precise centering but also durable use.

Note: Please upgrade your Literadio 3/2 SE to the latest version before replacing it. For the Litereadio 2 SE without BETAFPV configurator supported sticker (old version Literadio 2 SE) and Literadio 2, upgrading the newest firmware is unavailable, which is not compatible with the hall gimbal and only supports the potentiometer gimbal.

### Potentiometer or Hall Gimbal

The main difference between the two gimbals is the sensor. The potentiometer gimbal contains several electronic components that increase or decrease the level of resistance when moving. Compared with potentiometer gimbals, hall gimbals use a magnetic system, which has no wear from physical contact.
