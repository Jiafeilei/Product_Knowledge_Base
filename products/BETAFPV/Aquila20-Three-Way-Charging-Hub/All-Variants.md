# BETAFPV Aquila20 Three-Way Charging Hub

## 产品介绍

The Aquila20 Three‑Way Charging Hub is designed to keep you flying longer by charging three Aquila20 batteries simultaneously. With PD 3.0 fast charging support, each port delivers up to 15W for a total output of 45W, delivering rapid, efficient power to all your packs. A built‑in smart chip automatically allocates charging port based on the adapter's power and safely powers down when charging is complete. All of this is packed into a compact, travel‑ready hub that lets you power up anywhere.

### Bullet Points

- Compact & Portable: Small, travel‑ready design for charging anywhere.

- 45W Total Output: PD 3.0 fast charging with up to 15W per port. Fully charges in 40min.

- Smart Power Distribution: Automatically allocate charging port based on the adapter's power.

- Charge 3 Batteries Simultaneously: Designed specifically for your Aquila20 batteries.
