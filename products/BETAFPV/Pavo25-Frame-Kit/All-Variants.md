# BETAFPV Pavo25 Frame Kit

## 产品介绍

Pavo25 frame is made of high-quality PA12 material with strong and durable injected molding, which grants more stable flight and no fear of damage.

### Bullet Point

- With the insert copper nut design on the main screw holes, only 6 screws are enough to assemble the whoop duct and drone, which is convenient to build. The Pavo25 frame sports a reasonable structure assembly design that is optimized to decrease complex components, making the build easy.

- Made of high-quality PA12 material and 2.5mm carbon fiber. With a strong and durable frame kit, the Pavo25 frame optimizes the vibration damping structure for the camera mount, allowing pilots to get an excellent experience.

- An innovative pusher frame with a duct design. It generates more lift for the whole quadcopter, improving the ability of endurance and propulsion.

- Equipped with a CNC Vista Adapter. It effectively provides the Caddx Vista HD Digital VTX with a good cool down by increasing the heat dissipation area, reducing the influence of heat on VTX.

### CNC Vista Adapter

CNC Vista Adapter effectively provides the Caddx Vista HD Digital VTX with a good cool down by increasing the heat dissipation area, reducing the influence of heat on VTX. It is easy to install and has two mounting angles, which can be mounted by the angle 0° or 45° depending on your needs.

Note: The CNC Vista Adapter is included in the frame kit, if you need to buy it separately, please click here.
