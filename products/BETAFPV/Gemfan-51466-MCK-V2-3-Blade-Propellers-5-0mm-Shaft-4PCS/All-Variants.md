# BETAFPV Gemfan 51466 MCK V2 3-Blade Propellers (5.0mm Shaft 4PCS)

## 产品介绍

The Gemfan 51466 MCK V2 3-Blade Propellers are designed for agile flight at high speed with its incredibly low weight of just 4.2g, providing a much better FPV racing flight. Perfect for any 5" quads and suitable for BETAFPV 2306 Brushless Motors.

### Specification

- Material: PC

- Weight: 4.2g

- Pitch: 3.6 inch

- Prop Dia: 131.8mm

- Center Thickness: 6.8mm

- Center Hole Dia: M5

- Recommended Motor: 2207-2306, BETAFPV 2306 Brushless Motors

- Color: Blue, Orange, Gray

### Package

- 2 * CW 51466 MCK V2 3-Blade Props  (5.0mm shaft)

- 2 * CCW 51466 MCK V2 3-Blade Props  (5.0mm shaft)
