# BETAFPV ND Filter ND8 ND16 for Turtle V2 / RunCam Split Mini 2

## 产品介绍

To eliminate the Jelly effect, Caddx made a ND filter for Ratel and Turtle V2. Meanwhile, ND filter will make footage look clean and clear. ND8 Filter is for cloudy day while ND16 Filter is for sunny day.

### Bullet Point

- Adapt Camera: Turtle V2 camera, RunCam Split Mini 2

- Applies slow shutter speed under strong sunlight exposure, reduces the amount of light reaching the film without affecting the color.

- Allows wider apertures, effectively decreasing depth of field.

- Neutral Density Filter ( ND8 ND16) Great for outdoor scenes in strong sunlight, the larger the ND number, the stronger the filtering effect and more suitable for the glare environment.
