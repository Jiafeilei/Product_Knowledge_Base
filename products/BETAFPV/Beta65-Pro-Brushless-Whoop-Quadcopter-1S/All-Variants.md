# BETAFPV Beta65 Pro Brushless Whoop Quadcopter (1S)

## 产品介绍

Beta65 Pro Brushless Whoop Quadcopter is discontinued now. If you are interested in the Pro Series drone, welcome to try the Beta85 Pro 2 Brushless Whoop Quadcopter.

If you are using EU-LBT firmware radio controller, you might find that you can't bind successfully to Beta65pro. Here are the solutions we provide for you. Thanks to Sebastian Abramowski for giving us the important feedback about binding to EU-LBT firmware radio controller.

This is the tinier 1S brushless drone with 0802 motors and 65mm frame.

### Specification

- Frame Wheelbase: 65mm

- Propeller: 31mm 4-blades propellers

- Motors: 0802 17500KV

- Flight Controller: F4 1S AIO Brushless Flight Controller

- Firmware version: betaflight_matekf411rx_3.5.0

- ESC: 5A BLHeli ESC (integrated with FC)

- Camera & VTX: M01 AIO Camera 5.8G VTX

- Battery: 300mAh 1S 30C Battery

- Receiver: Built in SPI Frsky receiver (D8 & D16) / SPI Futaba receiver / Flysky receiver / Fullspeed DSMX receiver

- Weight: 23.29g (without battery)

- Flight time: 4 minutes with 300mAh battery（smooth flight in a windless environment）

Note: The updated 65pro can support both D8 & D16. Plus, please flash to the newest version Betaflight_3.5.0

Click here to get the Cli Dump of Beta65pro with F4 Brushless FC on the BETAFPV Support Freshdesk.

If you occur the issue of motor not spin up properly, go to BETAFPV Support Freshdesk for Troubleshooting - Motor Doesn't Spin.

This DSMX receiver is NOT compatible with Spektrum DXe radio transmitter and OrangeRX RF module.  For some Spektrum radio transmitter like DX6i/DX6e etc, the DSMX receiver may have a horrible range with your radio transmitter, please change the mode to DSM2 (DSMX in default). This issue is figured out by Micro Motor Warehouse.
