### 44% More Thrust, Superior Control

Powered by the Racing 0802 Motors (2026) featuring the 0.10mm stator core, the Meteor65 Pro II delivers exceptional efficiency and performance. Paired with lightweight GF 1409 3-Blade Props, it achieves a 5.17:1 thrust-to-weight ratio—generating 44% more thrust than previous propulsion combinations. Whether you're perfecting indoor cinematic shots or pushing freestyle limits, the Meteor65 Pro II provides smooth, fluid control with exceptional low-throttle torque for precise maneuvering in any environment.

###
Extended Sessions, Uncompromised Stability

The new Meteor65 Pro II Frame with an enlarged 70mm wheelbase, up from 66mm, delivers more stability. The battery slot is expanded to 16.1mm wide to accommodate larger, more powerful LAVA II 1S 480mAh batteries, extending flight time to 5:50 minutes—allowing pilots to enjoy longer sessions without compromise. The strengthened frame shares the same rugged durability as the Meteor75 Pro II frame, making it perfect for daily training, micro aerial photography, education, and competitions.

### Matrix 1S 5IN1 II Flight Controller

With the raw power of a 12A continuous and peak 18A ESC, the Matrix 1S 5IN1 II sufficiently meets the power demands of the Racing 0802 Motors, providing strong burst capability for the Meteor65 Pro II. Crash-proof confidence comes from 3-point mounting that creates cushioning space during impacts, while the 1.0mm thickened PCB and optimized layout further enhance crash resilience for long-lasting reliability.

### Ultra-Wide 173° FOV Air Camera

Equipped with the Air Camera in 16:9 aspect ratio, it delivers 33% more horizontal view than standard 4:3, paired with a 173° ultra-wide FOV—so you can see more through every corner and react faster. Inside, a new CMOS sensor ensures stable color rendition while running cooler, maintaining consistent performance flight after flight. The glass lens delivers sharper video quality and stands up to scratches better than plastic. Whether threading gates or diving gaps, the Air Camera gives you a clearer, wider, and truer view of the action.

### Meteor65 Pro II Developer Kit - Perfect for DIYers

The Meteor65 Pro II Developer Kit includes all the parts needed to build a Meteor65 Pro II quadcopter, with a spare set of propellers and a frame. The flight controller comes pre-flashed with the latest firmware and tuned for optimal performance. Pilots can enjoy building their own whoop by referring to the assembly diagram in the User Manual. Perfect for DIYers, pilots who enjoy building, and for training or teaching purposes.
