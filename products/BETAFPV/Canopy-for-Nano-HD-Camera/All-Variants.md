Adopt with injection molding technique, this canopy is more durable and flexible, specially designed for Betafpv Nano HD Camera. It is also compatible with all whoop drones which are installed with the Betafpv Nano HD Camera, like Beta65X HD and Meteor65 HD.

Adopt with injection molding technique, this canopy is more durable, anti-ruin and heat-resistant, provide well protection for the whoop drone
