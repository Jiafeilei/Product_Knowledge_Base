# BETAFPV BetaCube for Whoop Quads

## 产品介绍

Note: The camera that comes with the Betacube is Z02 AIO Camera (Wire-Connected Version).

This is the upgraded version of Betacube. A customed plastic holder is made in this newest version. Soldering is eliminated and it's all plug and play.

There is only one version for sales now. Here is the list:

- Frsky Rx+OSD version: F4+OSD+Frsky Rx flight controller.

The camera of the Betacube (Frsky version) is updated to Z02 camera (Wire-Connected Version)

How to Assemble the Cube

### Package

- 1 * BETAFPV brushed flight controller (All Connectors Soldered)

- 1 * BETAFPV Z02 AIO Camera VTX (All Connectors Soldered)

- 1 * Plastic Z02 Holder

- 4 * M1.2x4mm screws (Fit for stock Inductrix frame)

- 4 * M1.4x5mm screws (Fit for Beta75 etc 75mm frame)

- 4 * Anti-vibration ruber dampers
