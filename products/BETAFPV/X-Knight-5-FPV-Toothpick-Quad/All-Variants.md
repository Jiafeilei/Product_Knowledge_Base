# BETAFPV X-Knight 5'' FPV Toothpick Quad

## 产品介绍

X-Knight 5'' FPV Toothpick Quad is the first Sub-250g 6S FPV racing drone on the current market! It is ultralight 144g still below 250g with the battery, perfectly meet the current requested of FAA rules, make your flight easier without limitation. Equipped with 35A AIO FC and 550mAh 6S battery(not including), this toothpick quad is sure to give you that boost and performance you are looking for!

### Bullet Point

- X-Knight 5'' Quad is the first lightweight 6S racing drone released on BETAFPV. It is ultralight only 144g, which is below 250g even with the battery, perfectly meet the current requested of FAA rules, make your flight easier without limitation.

- It is the first BETAFPV racing drone that installs the F4 35A AIO FC! This FC supports a 6S power system and up to 35A continuous currents, brings the pilots an excellent racing flight experience.

- Equipped with 1805 1550KV motors and Gemfan 5125 propellers, X-Knight 5'' offers unparalleled execution in thrust and efficient control.

- Highly recommended for the 550mAh 6S battery, it will give your drone more power and speed.

- Assembled New V2 version of 5'' frame kit, which adopts T800 carbon fiber material becomes ultimate durability while maintaining minimal weight 26.44g.

### Flight Controller

Toothpick 35A AIO Brushless FC- The board is the first BLHeli_32 ESC 35A All-in-one toothpick FC on the current market. It can handle 35A continuous current(40A burst) and provide with massive levels of performance capability, but it still maintains ultralight only 7g. In addition, this board supports 2-6S Lipo battery, perfectly suitable for the 4''-5'' toothpick drone.

### Motors

1805 1550KV Brushless Motors is a lightweight motor that is best for 6S power. It only weights 16.1g for 1pc. Best matched with the Gemfan 5125 3-Blade props, the motor is very efficient in use and has an excellent racing flight experience. Combined with Toothpick 35A AIO Brushless FC, it will provide you enough output power.

### M02 5.8G VTX

M02 5.8G VTX board is a high-quality 5.8GHz VTX has 4 adjustable power including 25mW, 100mW, 200mW and 350mW. With 350mW output power, pilots can fly drones up to 600 meters away to explore more possibilities and fun.

- Only weight 1.7g.

- Smart Audio function is included

- Frequency: 5.8GHz 6 bands 40 channels

### Caddx Camera

The Caddx Baby Ratel has superb night vision performance in addition to an excellent daytime image with ultra-low latency. Latency is ultra-low at only 8ms. It is lighter but still maintains a high quality 1/1.18" HDR sensor, which provides up to 0.0001 Sensitivity of lux. It is very versatile in that you can switch between PAL and NTSC video and also between 4:3 and 16:9 video formats.

- Image Sensor: 1/1.8 Inch starlight HDR sensor

- Horizontal Resolution: 1200TVL

- FOV: 160 Degree

- Image: 16:9 & 4:3 (changeable)
