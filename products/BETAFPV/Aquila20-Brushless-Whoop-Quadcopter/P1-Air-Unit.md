### Learn with Confidence, Fly like a Pro

The Aquila20 is designed to grow with you. Powered by the self-developed flight controller, it features three distinct flight modes (N/S/M) that allow you to start with absolute stability and gradually unlock full acrobatic freedom as your skills develop.

Altitude Hold & Stable Hover (N Mode): Your confidence-building mode. A highly precise barometer allows the drone to lock its altitude and hover steadily in place automatically. It's the perfect safety net for taking your hands off the remote control, learning orientation, and ensuring a safe landing in emergencies.

Pro Modes (S & M Modes): For when you're ready to advance. S and M Modes disable all assists, giving experienced pilots complete, unfiltered control for flips, rolls, and precision maneuvering.

Turtle Mode: With this mode, if you flip over, a simple command on your remote control instructs the motors to flip the drone back onto its feet, so you can take off again instantly—no walking over to manually retrieve it.

### Know More About ArtLynk Protocol

The ArtLynk protocol is a low-latency, long-range communication protocol for drones, offering superior stability and range over traditional analog VTX. ArtLynk protocol enables real-time HD video at about 60ms latency from glass to glass, which is crucial for smooth flight control and high-quality FPV (First Person View). Here are the key features you may want to know.

- Artosyn Inside & KAP Supported: ArtLynk protocol uses the Artosyn's latest AR803X chipset, with core technology support provided by KAP (KAP company provides core technology support or product solution to all FPV companies and ensures compatibility for various FPV products using Artosyn chipsets).

-
Collaborative and Open Community: ArtLynk protocol is a collaborative FPV ecosystem not belonging to ONLY one company. Many FPV enterprises and teams would design and produce their own products based on the ArtLynk protocol, ensuring broad compatibility and fostering community.

The ArtLynk protocol supports the following devices, letting you customize your own build or simply bind and fly:

- BETAFPV VR04 HD goggles, Aquila20 P1, Meteor75 Pro P1

- HGLRC Draco

- More coming soon!

Firmware Compatibility Notes:

- To ensure seamless compatibility with third-party ArtLynk protocol equipments (such as HGLRC Draco), please verify that your BETAFPV devices are updated to firmware version V2.0.5 or later.

- The air unit needs to be updated via the ground station. First make sure the ground station and air unit are on the same firmware version, then proceed to update the air unit. And firmware V2.0.5 is not compatible with any V1 versions (V1.0.36 / V1.0.44).
