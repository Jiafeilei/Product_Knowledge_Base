# BETAFPV Aquila20 Exclusive Battery (2PCS)

## 产品介绍

The Aquila20 Exclusive Battery features an integrated smart management module, delivering reliable power with enhanced safety and control. Simply short press and then long press the power button to activate or disable the battery, preventing accidental drain during transport or storage. The button also provides real-time power status at a glance: fly with confidence when the glow is blue (75%+), and know it's time to land when it flashes red (<20%). And for the ultimate peace of mind, the battery intelligently enters sleep mode, automatically shutting off 30 seconds after disconnecting from the drone to protect itself and your gear. It’s not just a battery; it’s your smartest co-pilot.

### Bullet Points

- Optimized 10-Minute Flights: 1100mAh capacity specifically engineered to deliver maximum flight time for Aquila20.

- Smart Power Management: Short press + long press to securely enable or disable power with the integrated management module.

- Clear Battery Status: Blue (75%+), Green (50-75%), Yellow (20-50%), Red (20%) with flashing low-battery alert.

- Auto Sleep Mode: Automatically powers down after 30 seconds of disconnectivity from the drone.
