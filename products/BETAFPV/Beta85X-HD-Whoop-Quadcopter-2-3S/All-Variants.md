# BETAFPV Beta85X HD Whoop Quadcopter (2-3S)

## 产品介绍

We are sorry to announce that Beta85X HD is discontinued and will not be available for sales anymore. We recommend you buy its great alternative Beta85X HD Digital VTX.

NOTE: Beta85X 2-3S Quad DSMX and FCC versions will delivery USA frequency in default.

USA frequency table below. The frequency in red item is invalid.

Extreme power & speed compatible with 3S battery, BLHeli_32 16A ESC, EMAX Avan 2" props and 1105 6000KV motors, Beta85X definitely gives you more power and punch, the most thrilling flying experience you've ever had in flying whoop drones. You can rip it in the race, do cool freestyle and even dive a building.

Note: DO NOT FLY WITH 4S BATTERY.

### BULLET POINT

- Beta85X pushes the new era of brushless whoop drones to a higher level. With the new power system, you could fly real acro even with an HD DVR camera in racing or freestyle.

- With the high-performance STM32F405 processor FC boards and tuned Betaflight Ominibus 3.5.0 firmware. This is also the first 2" BNF drone that comes with BLHeli-32 ESC.

- Durable and light enough frame is customized for the Beta85X. Except for the traditional carbon fiber frame, another choice of plastic frame for your 2" props drone.

- Whole drone no soldering or tinkering necessary. Customized lightest 1105 6000KV motors with cable connectors. Just plug and play. EMAX Avan 2" prop is the best combo in this Beta85X drone.

- 450mAh 3S high rate battery is recommended by default. But pilots could also use their own batteries with battery strap. Optional and affordable.

In the Beta85X design, the part of HD DVR is independent from the drone. You could mount or remove the HD DVR part very easy. In default, the drone is come with CADDX Turtle V2 HD camera and the camera tilt is switchable from 25 to 35 degree.

The canopy for HD DVR camera is 3d printing with SLA, which is compatible with Runcam Split Mini 2.

Download the STL File for the HD camera mount.

### BLHELI_32 ESC

BLHeli_32 is the 3rd generation ESC firmware after BLHeli and BLHeli_S, which is written for ESC‘s that utilize 32-bit MCU. With the increase in processing power, faster input signals with lower latency and higher update rate would be possible in BLHeli_32, such as Dshot1200!

Beta85X is the first 2" BNF drone that supports BLHeli_32 ESC. With the other power system parts, EMAX Avan 2" propellers and 1105 6000KV motors, it gives you the highest performance in flying experience.

Know more about BLHeli_32 ESC from Oscarliang's blog.

### PROPS OUT

The Beta85X drone also comes with "props out" by default. Why we need "props out" direction?

Default Betaflight props direction, quadcopter dips and "washes out" in hard corners. With reversed props or "props out", no more dipping even in hardest cornering.

Know more about "Props Out".
