# BETAFPV Air HD Camera & Coaxial Cable

## 产品介绍

To achieve an ultra-light AIO FC, we developed matching components that carry the same weight-saving philosophy. The Air HD Camera weighs just 1.07g, thanks to a slim 0.8mm PCB and a compact design that doesn't sacrifice performance. Its companion coaxial cable is equally streamlined—using just 15 pins to connect the camera to the AIO board, compared to 26 pins on the standard P1 Air Unit cable. The result: less wiring, fewer pins, and a total cable weight of only 0.32g. Both the camera and cable are available separately, so you can build or replace with ease.
