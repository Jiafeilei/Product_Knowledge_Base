### Superior Performance & Durability

Featuring optimized aerodynamics with arm braces refined from 6 to 5 and a curved whoop duct design, the Meteor75 Pro II frame reduces weight and air resistance for improved flight efficiency. Weighing only 5.80g—nearly 2g lighter than the previous version—and built tough to withstand crashes, the frame offers an agile flying experience and exceptional durability, making Meteor75 Pro II the ideal choice for both indoor and outdoor flights.

### Powerful Propulsion System

Powered by the Freestyle 1102 Motors (2026), featuring an upgraded 2000 series alloy outer rotor housing material and a reinforced shaft design built to withstand the hardest impacts. Achieving 15% higher efficiency through improved slot fill factor, these motors deliver more power output and smoother throttle response when paired with GF 1811 3-Blade Props. With the LAVA II 1S 580mAh batteries, the Meteor75 Pro II achieves up to 10.5 minutes of flight time and a 6.33:1 thrust-to-weight ratio—giving you extended sessions to capture the perfect shot. For even longer flights, the 680mAh batteries extend air time up to 11.5 minutes.

### Matrix 1S 5IN1 II Flight Controller

With the raw power of a 12A continuous and peak 18A ESC, the Matrix 1S 5IN1 II sufficiently meets the power demands of the Freestyle 1102 Motors, providing strong burst capability for the Meteor75 Pro II. Crash-proof confidence comes from 3-point mounting that creates cushioning space during impacts, while the 1.0mm thickened PCB and optimized layout further enhance crash resilience for long-lasting reliability.

### Ultra-Wide 173° FOV Air Camera

Equipped with the Air Camera in 16:9 aspect ratio, it delivers 33% more horizontal view than standard 4:3, paired with a 173° ultra-wide FOV—so you can see more through every corner and react faster. Inside, a new CMOS sensor ensures stable color rendition while running cooler, maintaining consistent performance flight after flight. The glass lens delivers sharper video quality and stands up to scratches better than plastic. Whether threading gates or diving gaps, the Air Camera gives you a clearer, wider, and truer view of the action.

### Meteor75 Pro II Developer Kit - Perfect for DIYers

The Meteor75 Pro II Developer Kit includes all the parts needed to build a Meteor75 Pro II quadcopter, with a spare set of propellers and a frame. The flight controller comes pre-flashed with the latest firmware and tuned for optimal performance. Pilots can enjoy building their own whoop by referring to the assembly diagram in the User Manual. Perfect for DIYers, pilots who enjoy building, and for training or teaching purposes.
