### Perfect Exposure, Every Frame

- ND 8/16/32 filters: Give you complete control over light, reducing glare and overexposure to deliver smooth, cinematic footage in bright environments. Perfect for achieving natural motion blur and balanced exposure.

- UV filter: Effectively minimize the blue tone caused by ultraviolet rays, delivering clearer, more vibrant, and true-to-life images. They also protect your camera lens from dirt, sand, and scratches, keeping your shots pristine and your gear protected.

### How To Install and Remove?

To install the filter: Align it with the lens position and clip it into the frame vertically until it securely locks in place.

To remove the filter: Grip the lens mount with your thumb and gently pull it out.

Pro Tip: If the filter feels loose or shakes after installation, use a heat gun to warm the camera housing. Then carefully clamp the camera buckle with a tool until it cools down, restoring it to its original size for a snug fit.
