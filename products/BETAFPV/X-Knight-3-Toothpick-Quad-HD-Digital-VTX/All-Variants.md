# BETAFPV X-Knight 3'' Toothpick Quad  (HD Digital VTX)

## 产品介绍

X-knight 3'' Digital VTX comes with a small size 140mm wheelbase, which gives the drone more abilities to avoid obstacles and best flight operation. It weighs only 126.7g without battery, but its performance is still powerful enough to support all kinds of FPV freestyle tricks. Come with the Caddx Vista kit, pilots can get a long transmission range with stable and clear HD video. If you are looking for a drone that combines HD video recording and best FPV flight, X-Knight 3'' HD Digital VTX Toothpick Quad will be your best choice.

### Bullet Point

- X-Knight 3'' HD Digital VTX Toothpick Quad is a lightweight 4S quadcopter. Power and strong enough as a 3inch quadcopter, it can easily avoid obstacles and also do various acrobatic motions smoothly.

- X-Knight 3'' Toothpick Quad (HD Digital VTX) equipped with Caddx Vista VTX, which can record 720P 120fps video. With this vista kit, the max range is up to 4 km, pilots can get a stable and clean HD video on the flight.

-
Come with F4 AIO 12A Toothpick FC, integrated with a Betaflight MATEKF411RX processor FC, Betaflight OSD and 2-4S 12A ESC, support 4S battery for the fast-flying experience, it can flight all kind of FPV freestyle tricks.

- Equipped with 1404 3800KV brushless motors and Gemfan 3016 propellers, it is a perfect match to offer pilots an agile flight at high speed, which will give you super-efficient flight experience.

-
Assembled 3'' frame kit, which adopts T800 carbon material becomes ultimate durability while maintaining minimal weight 22.79g.

### Caddx Vista

The Caddx Vista HD digital video transmitter can work with DJI FPV camera, goggles, and remote controller. The total weight (with camera and antenna) is 35g only, about 20g lighter than the DJI FPV unit. Besides, it is smaller and designed to suit for whoop drones. With this vista kit, the max range is up to 4 kilometers. BETAFPV mini canopy V3  is suitable for the Caddx Vista greatly. With injection molded process and PE material. The canopy will perfectly protect the Caddx Vista camera.

### Flight Controller

F4 AIO 12A Toothpick Brushless FC- Integrated with a Betaflight MATEKF411RX processor FC, Betaflight OSD and 2-4S 12A ESC, support 4S battery for the fast-flying experience.

### Motors & Props

1404 3800KV Brushless Motors is a lightweight motor that is suitable for a 3-4inch drone. It only weighs 9.66g/pc. Perfectly matched with the Gemfan 3016 3-Blade Props, the motor is very efficient in use and has an excellent racing flight experience. Combined with Toothpick 12A AIO Brushless FC, it will provide you enough output power.

### X-Knight Carbon Fiber 3'' Frame Kit

X-Knight Carbon Fiber 3'' Frame Kit which adopts T800 carbon material becomes ultimate durability while maintaining minimal weight 22.79g. It consists of arms, bottom plate and top plate, which allows you to replace the damaged part. Meanwhile, the top plate comes with press nuts to make the installation process easier.
