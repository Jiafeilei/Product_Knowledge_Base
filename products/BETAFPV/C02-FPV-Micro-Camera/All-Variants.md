# BETAFPV C02 FPV Micro Camera

## 产品介绍

C02 Micro Camera is the lightweight camera customized for those weight sensitive micro whoop drones, like Meteor65, Beta65S, Beta85 pro2, HX100SE. Adapted a 1/4'' CMOS sensor, 2.1mm lens for  1200TVL resolution and excellent latency while minimizing the weight to 1.4g. Packed with the micro canopy, protect your camera well in a hard crash.

### Plug-N-Play Connector

C02 camera features a JST-0.8 connector, special design in plug-and-play, makes it easy to install without soldering. Suitable for Cetus seris drones, BETAFPV F4 1S AIO FC, BETAFPV M03 VTX, and NBD BeeBrain flight controllers.

### Bullet Points

- Miniature, and lightweight design, weight at only 1.4g. Best match with 5.8G A01 VTX on micro whoop drone.

- 1200TVL resolution and 1/4" CMOS sensor ensure a high-quality and clear image

- Global WDR camera, 16:9 aspect ratio, shoot clear dynamic image even in low light condition

- 2.1mm lens, FOV 160° beyond the wide-angle for recording more wide and beautiful scenes

- The package comes with an injection molding canopy, more durable, which is the best combo with C02 camera
