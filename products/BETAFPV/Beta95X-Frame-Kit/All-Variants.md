# BETAFPV Beta95X Frame Kit

## 产品介绍

Do we need a whoop frame that could support 2.5inch props? Many pilots gave us feedback and advice since the early of 2019, named 95X. The project of a whoop drone with 2.5" props started in BETAFPV R&D department then. In the past year, we doubt the real requirement for duct on such a large drone. Then delay, and delay again.

-- On Facebook PowerWhoop Group by Kevin Bernitz

I reached out to Betafpv about this and didn't get a response. I had a doinker frame that I had upscaled to 2.5" with today's electronics and props it would be awesome. My version used 30x30 electronics  -- By Chris Davis

You know would actually make it fly much better .... no ducts. What’s the point of having them at this size?  -- By Daniel Sugano

Kevin .. I'm with you. I feel a lot better flying the whoops around.. I've been hit with whoops, not so bad and seen the bloody mess from a 3 inch hitting a guy in the back.. yeah, I prefer the whoops ;)  -- By Chris Parry

......

Until 2 months ago, the requirement of a whoop drone that could carry a 35g VTX and still could fly acrobatic. Yes, now we need a 95X frame for 2.5inch props, even the real wheelbase is 100mm.

### Upgrade Your 85X

The new 95X frames are compatible with all the parts on your 85X drone.

Pilots could use the original electronic parts and motors on your 85X drones. Only a new frame and some 2.5" props you could experience the great flying experience on a 95X drone.

Besides, if you have a 12A or 20A whoop AIO FC board, just have a try on this new frame.

### Bullet Point

- The Carbon Brace can reinforce the drone, avoid instability caused by too strong power. It will increase the stiffness and support across the frame. Prevents a lot of damage and still get solid flight time.

- The Beta95x frame can fit the 110x brushless motors like 1105 brushless motors perfectly. It comes with an LED holder and antenna holder, convenient for installing an LED board and antenna.

- Shipped with plastic durable package, protects the package from deformation during the shipping process.

### Specification

The specification of the Beta95X frame.

- Color: Black

- Material: Plastic

- Wheelbase: 100mm

- Motor mounting hole: for 110x motors (like 1105 5000KV motors)

- FC mounting hole: fully compatible with whoop style FC board mounting pattern, 26mm*26mm

- LED holder and antenna holder available

- Weight: 13.5g

The specification of the stiffener brace.

- Material: T700 carbon fiber

- Weight：6.7g

- Thickness: 2.5mm

- Suitable for Beta95X frame

### Recommend Parts

- FC / ESC: F4 V2 4S F405 FC and BLHeli_32 16A ESC / 12A Whoop AIO FC board / 20A Whoop AIO FC board

- Motors: 1105 Motors / 1204 Motors / 1106 Motors

- Batteries: 450mAh 4S battery / 450mAh 3S battery

- Props: Gemfan 2540 3-Blade Props

- Canopy: 4K Canopy or Mini Canopy

High recommend the mini canopy and Caddx Vista VTX for this frame.
