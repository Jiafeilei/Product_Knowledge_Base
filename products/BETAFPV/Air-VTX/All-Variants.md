# BETAFPV Air VTX

## 产品介绍

The Air VTX is an analog video transmitter built for pilots who demand simplicity, weight savings, and race-ready performance. Weighing just 0.6g and measuring only 14×10.5mm, it delivers clean 25mW output with PIT mode support—everything you need for competitive flying. Pair it with the Matrix 1S 4IN1 FC for greater flexibility and a cleaner custom build.

### Bullet Points

- Ultra‑Lightweight & Compact: At just 0.6g and a compact 14×10.5mm, ideal for weight‑conscious racing builds.

- Race‑Ready 25mW Output: Provides clean, reliable video transmission for short‑range racing.

- PIT Mode Support: Low‑power mode, allowing you to power up without interfering with other pilots.

- FC Pairing: Use with the Matrix 1S 4IN1 FC, offering greater flexibility and clean integration for custom builds.

### Specifications

- Operating voltage: 5V

- Operating current: 150 mA (25mW)

- VTX internal size: 14*10.5mm

- Antenna connector: U.FL

- Output power: 25/PIT

- Frequency: 5.8GHz 48 channels, with Raceband: 5658-5917MHz

- Channel SEL: SmartAudio 2.0

- Video format: PAL/NTSC

- Modulation type: FM

- Frequency control: PLL

- All harmonic: Max -50dBm

- Frequency stability: ±100KHz (Typ.)

- Frequency precision: ±200KHz (Typ.)

- Channel carrier error: ±1.5dB

- Antenna port: 50 Ω

- Operating temperature: -10℃~+80℃

- Weight: 0.6g (antenna excluded)

## 技术参数

| Action | Function | LED Behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Short press | Change band / channel | Flash count increases |
| Press and hold (1 sec) | Enter band / channel settings | Blue LED flashes: band selection mode Green LED flashes: channel selection mode |
| No operation for 15 sec after power‑on | Boot locked (settings protected) | Green and blue LEDs flash simultaneously; the flash pattern corresponds to the current band and channel |
| Press and hold (5-10 sec) | Boot unlocked (settings editable) | Green and blue LEDs change from simultaneous flashing to a single‑color flash pattern |
