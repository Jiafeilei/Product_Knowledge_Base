# BETAFPV Canopy for Mini Camera

## 产品介绍

Currently, both MINI V2 and MINI V3 canopies suit for Beta95X, Beta85X, Beta75X and Beta75 pro 2 frame. MINI V3 canopy is suitable for all mini cameras which are the 19*19mm or 20*20mm FPV cameras, like Caddx Vista or DJI FPV unit camera, Caddx Turtle V2, RunCam Racer 3.

### Comparision between V2 and V3

MINI V3 canopy has a holder hole in the front, but not on MINI V2 canopy. Like what we say, MINI V2 canopy is for Caddx Turtle V2 camera while MINI V3 canopy is for Caddx Vista digital VTX.

-
MINI V2 canopy is used on Beta75X HD and Beta85X HD.

- MINI V3 canopy is used on TWIG XL, X-Knight 4 and Beta95X.

The below image is V3 blue canopy mounted with the Caddx Vista VTX.
Additional screws are needed, please check the Caddx Vista page for help.

### PE Material

More soft and anti-ruin, effectively prevent canopy from breaking.
If you use other screws to install the DVR board, be careful not to damage the electronic components, otherwise will cause the camera to be abnormal.

### Specification

- Item: MINI V2/V3 canopy

- Material: PE

- Weight: 3.8g

- Adapted Camera: Caddx Turtle V2, Caddx Vista or DJI FPV unit camera, RunCam Racer 3

- Adapted Frame: Beta95X Frame, Beta85X Frame, Beta75X Frame, Beta75 Pro 2 Frame
