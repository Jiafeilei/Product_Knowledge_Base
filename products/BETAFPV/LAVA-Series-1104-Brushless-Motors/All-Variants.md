# BETAFPV LAVA Series 1104 Brushless Motors

## 产品介绍

The LAVA Series 1104 Brushless Motors, available in 5500KV for 4S and 7200KV for 3S, are designed specifically for 2.2" quads with smoother torque output performance. Combined with the Gemfan D2.2 3-Blade Propellers, they enable pilots to execute swift, agile, and smooth freestyle maneuvers with precision. This powerful combination ensures ample creative space for cinewhoop pilots to explore and capture the most imaginative aerial feats, easily meeting professional film creation needs while leading the 2.2" cinewhoop class to new heights.

### Bullet Points

- Unleash the power of freestyle with the LAVA Series 1104 motors, precision-engineered for 3S and 4S 2.2'' quads. Experience a quantum leap in performance, taking the Pavo20 Pro II freestyle experience to new heights.

- With the Gemfan D2.2 3-Blade Propellers, the max thrust can reach more than 230g, achieving a thrust-to-weight ratio greater than 5:1, delivering a faster and more agile freestyle experience.

- Paired with the above GF props, the force efficiency is as high as 4.7g/w when flying at low throttle, and even at full throttle load conditions, it can reach 1.9g/w, providing an efficient propulsion system for the quad.
