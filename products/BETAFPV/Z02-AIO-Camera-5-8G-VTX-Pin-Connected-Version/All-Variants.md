# BETAFPV Z02 AIO Camera 5.8G VTX (Pin-Connected Version)

## 产品介绍

Note: Z02 camera (Pin Connected Version) is now off shelf. The Wire Connected Version is for sales now.

Z02 AIO camera VTX is the newest video transmitter by BETAFPV. It has 2 great improvement since. First, switchable power output between 0mW, 25mW and 200mW. Second, Smart Audio is included and pilots could control the VTX via your radio transmitter.

### FEATURE

- Switchable power includes 0mW, 25mW and 200mW.

- Smart Audio function is included in this AIO Z02 camera. The flight controller can communicate with the video transmitter using the audio line in. It allows the flight controller to know what frequency the vtx is using, and to change it.

- Fixed 20° tilt camera mount. At this angle the pilot can still see everything ahead in full-blown fast forward flight.

- Dipole whip high sensitivity antenna

###

### SPECIFICATION

- Output power: 0mW, 25mW and 200mW (Adjustable)

- Supply Voltage: 2.9-5.5V

- Size : 27*17*14 mm

- Weight: 4.7g

- Antenna: Omnidirectional ANT

- Frequency: 5.8GHz 6 bands 48 channels, with Raceband: 5362~5945MHz

- TV System: NTSC

- Field of view: 100-120 degrees with 1/4 lens.

### How to Change Frequency & Channel & Power

- Frequency group A~F selected still touch up to 2 secs

- Channel ch1~ch8 selected with short touch.

- Power switch with long touch up to 5 secs.

In default, the connectors of the Z02 is compatible with BETAFPV FC boards like F3 V2, F4 etc.

### PACKAGE

- 1 * BETAFPV Z02 FPV Camera

- 1 * Holder

- 2 * Head pins (Using to connect with newest BETAFPV FC boards)
