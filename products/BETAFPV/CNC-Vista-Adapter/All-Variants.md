# BETAFPV CNC Vista Adapter

## 产品介绍

CNC Vista Adapter effectively provides the Caddx Vista HD Digital VTX with a good cool down by increasing the heat dissipation area, reducing the influence of heat on VTX.

### Bullet Point

- The CNC Vista Adapter is designed for Caddx Nebula Pro Nano Vista Kit and can be mounted on any whoop frame available in the market like OddityRC, IFlight, or GEPRC whoop frame. It reserves the 20*20mm mounting hole and is also compatible with analog VTX.

- It is easy to install and has two mounting angles. The CNC Vista Adapter can be mounted by 0° or 45° depending on your needs.

- Highly increasing the heat dissipation area, the CNC Vista Adapter provides a good cool down for VTX, which is important for long-distance flight.

### Specification

- VTX Mounting Hole Size: 20*20mm

- FC Mounting Hole Size: 36.4*36.4mm

- Installing Angle: 0° or 45°

- Mounting Screw Size: M2*12

- Recommend Quadcopter:  Pavo25 Whoop Quadcopter

### Package

- 1 * CNC Vista Adapter
