# BETAFPV Gemfan 40mm 2-Blade Propellers (1.0mm Shaft 4PCS)

## 产品介绍

Designed for the 40mm whoop drone, more power, higher efficiency, and better control. Ultra-durable blade, these 40mm props are made of high-quality PC material with the best efficiency and durability. Gemfan 40mm 2-Blade Props are suitable for 1mm shaft motors like 08028 12000KV or 0802SE Brushless Motors

### Recommended Parts

- Frame: Meteor75 Micro Brushless Whoop Frame

- Motor: 0702 Brushless Motors, 0802SE Brushless Motors

- Quadcopter: Air75 II, Meteor75, Meteor75 (HD), Air75

### Specification

- Color: White, Transparent

- Product material: PC

- Inner diameter: 1.0mm Shaft

- Weight: 1.02g/set (White), 0.99g/set (Transparent)

### Package

- 2 * CW 2-blade 40mm Props  (1.0mm shaft)

- 2 * CCW 2-blade 40mm Props (1.0mm shaft)
