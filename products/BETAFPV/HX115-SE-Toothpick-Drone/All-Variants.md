# BETAFPV HX115 SE Toothpick Drone

## 产品介绍

The HX115 SE Toothpick Drone is an exceptional 3'' ultralight drone with multiple enhancements over its 2021 version. It boasts the new F4 1S 12A AIO FC 2022 and an upgraded 2S power source for a more powerful experience. Equipped with 1103 | 8000KV motors and HQ 3020 2-blade Props, resulting in extended flight time from 5 mins to 12 mins. The Upgraded M03 25-350mW 5.8G VTX, with up to 350mW transmission power, keeps stable transmission of images over long distances and in complex environments. Its lightweight design and smooth flight capabilities embody the perfect balance of extreme lightness and performance, satisfying pilots' pursuit of excellence.

### Bullet Point

- Upgraded 2S propulsion system and 1103 | 8000KV motors improved flight stability and thrust, and only weigh 40.76g.

- Equipped with HQ 3020 2-blade props, it generates high thrust at high speed while consuming less power, resulting in a longer flight time of 12 mins.

- The new F4 1S 12A ELRS V2.2/Frsky V1.2 FC provides more power, faster response time, and a more precise control experience.

- Upgraded M03 VTX with 350mW image transmission power ensures stable transmission in complex or long-distance environments.

- The 1.45g C03 FPV micro camera captures high-quality and clear video featuring a FOV 160° beyond wide angle, 1200TVL resolution, and a 1/3" CMOS sensor.

- The detachable black Micro canopy 2022 provides easy replacement and maintenance, providing more installation options and better adaptability for various 3'' toothpick drones freestyle.

### Flight Controller

The F4 1S 12A AIO FC 2022 is applied to HX115 SE Toothpick Drone. Both SPI ELRS 2.4G and SPI Frsky can be used as PNP due to extra solder pads for the external receiver. The SPI Frsky version also can be switched to the Futaba SFHSS protocol through the Betaflight configurator. The ESC on the new board powered by BB51 hardware comes with Bluejay 48K ESC firmware, allowing the 1103 | 8000KV motors to give full play to the efficiency of power. We update the gyro to BOSH BMI270 for better performance and stability. This FC can respond to operating instructions faster, providing a more precise racing control experience.

Note: F4 1-2S 12A AIO SPI ELRS 2.4G comes with the default firmware betaflight_4.3.1_BETAFPVF4SX1280, which supports ELRS V2.0. ONLY the Betaflight firmware 4.3.0 and up start to support this BMI270 gyro. Please check the below link to download the firmware.
