# BETAFPV DSMX Receiver for Micro Drone

## 产品介绍

This is a tiny DSMX receiver for micro brushed / brushless drones. This is also the stock receiver for BETAFPV BNF 1S brushless Beta65 Pro / Beta75 Pro drones.

Tip: NOT compatible with Spektrum DXe radio transmitter and OrangeRX RF module.

### Specification

- Input power: 3.0-3.6V

- Response time: 22ms/11ms

- Resolution: 2048

- Display method: LED

- Working current: 60MA

- Size: 16mm * 10mm

- Weight: 0.6 g

- Receive sensitivity: -108DBM

- Delay: <15ms

- Antenna length: 26MM Dual Antenna

- Data format: SBUS

- Distance: >500m

- Channels: support 6-8 channels

- Compatible Transmitters: DSM2 / DSMX series of remote transmitter

About how to bind to your Spektrum radio transmitter, please check here.

This DSMX receiver is NOT compatible with Spektrum DXe radio transmitter and OrangeRX RF module. For some Spektrum radio transmitter like DX6i/DX6e etc, the DSMX receiver may have a horrible range with your radio transmitter, please change the mode to DSM2 (DSMX in default). This issue is figured out by Micro Motor Warehouse.

About how to bind the radio, please check Support Helpdesk.
