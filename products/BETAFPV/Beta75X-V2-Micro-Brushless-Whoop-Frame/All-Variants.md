# BETAFPV Beta75X V2 Micro Brushless Whoop Frame

## 产品介绍

Compared with the Beta75X frame, the Beta75X V2 frame only weighs 5.6g, It is lighter and the gravity center of the V2 frame is more concentrated. Increased the height of the frame loop, props can be completely wrapped by the protective ring. A better choice for a 75mm brushless whoop drone. What's more, the Beta75X V2 Frame is specially designed with two different motor mounting holes that support 1102 and 1103 brushless motors.

###
Bullet Point

-
The Beta75X V2 frame only weighs 5.6g, it is lighter and the whole drone’s gravity center more concentrated than the Beta75X V1. Meanwhile, the motor mount position uses a quadrilateral stretch structure, makes the frame more durable and stable.

- Increased the height of the frame loop, props are completely wrapped by the protective ring, more effectively protected the propellers.

- Designed two different motor mounting holes that support 1102 and 1103 brushless motors at the same time.

- With a 26mm*26mm FC mounting columns can match whoop style flight controllers like F4 AIO 12A FC. The battery slot fits BETAFPV 300mAh 2S and 300mAh 3S-S battery.

###  Specification

- Material: Plastic

- Color: Black / White

- Weight: 5.6g

- Diagonal motor to motor distance: 75mm

- Motor mounting hole: for 110x series motors

- FC mounting hole: 26mm x 26mm, fully compatible with whoop style FC board mounting pattern

- Battery slot size: 6.8x16.1mm

###

### Recommended Parts

- Propellers: 40mm 4-blade props (1.5mm shaft hole)

- Motors: 110x brushless motors, like 1103 11000KV motors

- Battery: 3S 300mAh Battery / 2S 300mAh Battery

- FC & ESC: F4 1~2S AIO Brushless FC, F4 AIO 12A FC

- 1 * Beta75X V2 Whoop Frame for 75mm 3S whoop drone
