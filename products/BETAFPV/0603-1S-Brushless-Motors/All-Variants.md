# BETAFPV 0603 1S Brushless Motors

## 产品介绍

The lightest 1.8g Betafpv brushless motors- 0603 19000/16000KV motors.

0603 19000KV motor will give stronger power performance compared with 16000KV option, while power by 16000KV will bright longer flight time.

PARTS UPDATED

-
The cables are unified in black color, no need to distinguish between CW and CCW motors.

- Lead wire soldered to the PCB, which allows you to repair it easier once disconnection.

The performance of 65mm 1S brushless drone has gone up a new level this winter. What is your exception for this drone? Aggressive power and faster speed, or longer flying time? Here is the comparison of different motors in Meteor65 Drone. Choose your favorite type of motor to build your own drone.

### Recommended Parts

- Drone: Meteor65

- Propellers: 31mm Propellers

- Batteries: 300mAh 1S 30C Battery / BT2.0 300mah 1S Battery

- Frame: Beta65 Pro frame / Meteor65 Frame

- Drone: Meteor65 / Beta65 Pro Whoop Quad

Really not recommend the 0703 motors with a 65mm frame ( like Beta65 Pro frame or BWhoop B06 etc). Why? The combo of 0703 motors and 31mm props (for 65mm frame) has a really low efficiency. Really not power enough. For the 65mm 1S brushless drone, high recommend these 0603 motors.

Here is the testing result of different motors and frames combo.

-  Throttle value means the position when the drone hovers in the air.

- Flying time is the time from OSD voltage value 4.3V->2.8V.

- All tested with 4-blades props. 31mm props or 40mm props.

- All throttle values are tested on full charged 550mAh batteries.

- The KV value of 0703/0603 motors are both 15000KV.
