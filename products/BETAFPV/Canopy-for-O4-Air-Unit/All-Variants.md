# BETAFPV Canopy for O4 Air Unit

## 产品介绍

Tailored for whoop drones, the Canopy for O4 Air Unit integrates BETAFPV's signature micro canopy design with optimized inner space and mounting holes for DJI O4 Air Unit's shock-absorbing components. Featuring an adjustable camera angle from 10° to 35°, it ensures perfect HD footage without capturing the frame or top cover. With standard 25.5mm x 25.5mm mounting holes, it also supports DIY customization, giving pilots the flexibility to build their ideal whoops for any flying scene.

### Bullet Points

- Optimized for DJI O4 Air Unit: Incorporates Shock-absorbing Components for stable HD footage.

- Adjustable Camera Angle: 10° to 35° range to suit various shooting scenes.

- DIY-Friendly: Standard 25.5mm x 25.5mm mounting holes for customization flexibility.

- Classic BETAFPV Style: Retains the iconic Micro canopy design while protecting the camera.
