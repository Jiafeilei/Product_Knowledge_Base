# BETAFPV Pavo Series Battery Strap (2PCS)

## 产品介绍

The Pavo Series Battery Strap is a vital accessory for your drone. Designed to tackle the risks associated with high-speed maneuvers, sharp turns, and bumps during flight, this strap ensures your battery remains securely fastened to the drone body. With 3 sizes available - 130mm, 180mm, and 220mm - you can select the ideal fit for your specific quadcopter model. Say farewell to battery-related concerns and soar with unwavering confidence, thanks to the Pavo Series Battery Strap.

- 14*130mm Battery Strap suitable for 2~4S 450/650mAh battery

- 14*220mm Battery Strap suitable for 4~6S 1000~1500mAh battery
