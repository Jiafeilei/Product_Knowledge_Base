# BETAFPV 65mm Micro Whoop Frame for 6x15mm Motors (Eachine E010 Version)

## 产品介绍

These frames are a great alternative to the stock Blade Inductrix airframe - Eachine E010 black durable frames.

They are a big heavier - 5.05g opposed to the stock airframe's weight of 3.22g, but they are considerably more durable and have a nice little spot to tuck the wires as they run from the motors to the flight controller. Some people getting into flying acro use this airframe to have additional strength in the inevitable crashes.

Tips: These frames will have to be modified to carry Whoop style batteries.

- 1 * 65mm Micro Whoop Frame (Eachine E010 Version)
