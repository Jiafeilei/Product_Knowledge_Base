# BETAFPV H01 AIO Camera 5.8G 25mW VTX

## 产品介绍

Note:The position of the red & black wires on the H01 camera have been changed.

### Bullet Points

- Light weight only 3.6g, small size with 47 * 14.8 * 12.5 mm.

- Supporting touch switch select channel and frequency group, supporting NTSC/PAL switch.

- 8 LEDs display channel and 6 LEDS display frequency group information, 1 LED display PAL/NTSC information.

### How to Switch Channel, Frequency Group and NTSC/PAL Mode

- Short press on the button to change channel.

- Long press on the button to change frequency group.

- Longer press on the button to switch NTSC/PAL mode. (Green light on means NTSC and green light off means PAL mode.)

Note: this camera doesn't support vertical image convertion.

- Output power: 25mW

- Weight: 3.6g
