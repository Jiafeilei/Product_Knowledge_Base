# BETAFPV X-Knight Canopy

## 产品介绍

﻿X-Knight canopy (AKA HD Digital VTX canopy) is specially designed for the Digital VTX camera, adapted for all Caddx Vista Camera. It doesn't only look like an x-knight helmet, but also gives the camera the best protection like a knight. This canopy can fit all of BETAFPV 75mm, 85mm,95mm brushless whoop frame. What'more, with the heightened mount, this canopy can suitable for all TWIG series and X-Knight series digital VTX drones.

The image of this canopy used on the TWIG XL 3.5" drone.

### Bullet Point

- HD Digital VTX canopy is specially designed for Caddx Vista and Nebula Nano camera. Installed with Nebula needs an extra camera mount to lock.

- With a brand new appearance, which looks like an x-knight helmet, brings the drone more freshness and more mystery.

- The best replacement for the canopy for Beta95X digital VTX, X-Knight3'' HD digital VTX and X-Knight5 HD digital VTX.

This HD Digital VTX Canopy can fit all of BETAFPV 75mm, 85mm,95mm brushless whoop frame. What'more, with the heightened mount, it also suitable for all TWIG, X-knight series digital VTX drones.
