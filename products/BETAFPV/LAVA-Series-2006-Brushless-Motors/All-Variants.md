Easily meet competitively challenging drone competitions and professionally shot film creation needs, bringing you a superb flight experience and high-quality image output, leading the 6S 3.5'' quads to a smoother and more stable flight level.

- Specifically designed for 6S 3.5'' quads, this motor offers high agility and acceleration and features a high-performance 2400KV motor. Pilots enjoy flexible control and get more room for additional payload capacity.
