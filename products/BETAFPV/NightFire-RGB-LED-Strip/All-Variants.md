# BETAFPV NightFire RGB LED Strip

## 产品介绍

Elevate your nighttime flying with the NightFire RGB LED Strip—the same premium lighting featured in the Pavo20 Pro NightFire Limited Edition. Designed for enhanced visibility and style, it features vibrant RGB LEDs that bring your whoop to life with a stunning spectrum of colors and dynamic display modes. Easily switch between 10 preset lighting effects and 7 colors by pressing the buttons. The NightFire RGB LED Strip is built to shine brilliantly against the night sky. Light up your flight path like never before.

### Specifications

RGB Controller

- Input Voltage: 6V-18V (2S-4S)

- Output Voltage: 5V

- Output Current: 3A

- Beads: WS2812B RGB colorful LED

- Bead Count: Max. 128

- Input Connector: GH1.25mm 3-Pin

- Input/Output Connector: SH1.0mm 3-Pin
