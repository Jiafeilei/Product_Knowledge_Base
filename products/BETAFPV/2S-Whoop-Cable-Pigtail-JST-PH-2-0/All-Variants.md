# BETAFPV 2S Whoop Cable Pigtail (JST-PH 2.0)

## 产品介绍

This double connectors cable is just for 2S whoop drone. So pilots could still use the same 1S whoop style batteries to fly the Beta65X or Beta75X. As show below.

JST-PH 2.0 connector, AKA PowerWhoop connector, is the standard connector for whoop drones nowadays. The metariel of power cable is silicone. Thickness is 24AWG.

### Specifications

- Connector : Female JST-PH 2.0, AKA PowerWhoop Female

- Length: 55mm (Not included the plug)
