# BETAFPV Storage Case for 65/75mm Micro Drone

## 产品介绍

This case is designed for carrying a 65-75mm drone, it is made of EVA material, which is sturdy, durable and waterproof (except zipper part). Come with ample space for storing canopy, props, chargers, and batteries, etc. With this case, you're ready to grab your gear and go flying whenever you want, without having to carry around a bunch of different containers.

### Bullet Point

-
Come with a sturdy and durable EVA material, this case is water-proof(except zipper part) and resistant to impact damage.

- Ample space for storing the canopies, props, chargers, and batteries, etc.

- Built-in grid space, easy to store the small accessories.

- Wide-applicability, this case can not only suit for BETAFPV Meteor series/65X/65S and other whoop drones, it is also suitable for most brands 65/75mm FPV drones.
