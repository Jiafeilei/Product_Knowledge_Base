# BETAFPV BT2.0 Female-Male Adapter Cable

## 产品介绍

Exclusive Female-Male Adapter Cable for Aquila16 FPV Kit, a highly recommended accessory for Aquila16 pilots. With only 2 charging adapter cables included in the kit, meeting the extra charging demands can be challenging. However, this adapter cable provides a convenient solution, allowing you to charge effortlessly your Aquila16 Exclusive Battery directly using a 6 Ports 1S Battery Charger & Adapter or BT2.0 Battery Charger and Voltage Tester V2. This boosts charging efficiency, saving time and space, simplifying operations, and facilitating management. Don't miss out on this essential accessory to enhance the beginner Aquila16 flying experience.

- Item: BT2.0 Female-Male Adapter Cable

- Input Port: BT2.0 male connector (connect BT2.0 charger)
