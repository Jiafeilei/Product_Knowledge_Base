# BETAFPV Meteor75 Pro II Brushless Whoop Frame

## 产品介绍

The Meteor75 Pro II Frame is a next-generation whoop frame designed for superior flight performance, durability, and ease of use. Featuring optimized aerodynamics with arm braces refined from 6 to 5 and a curved whoop duct design, it reduces weight and air resistance for improved flight efficiency. With simplified installation through four shock-absorbing mounting holes and seamless Canopy II compatibility, the frame delivers stable, jello-free footage and effortless maintenance. Weighing only 5.80g—nearly 2g lighter than the previous version—and built tough to withstand crashes, the Meteor75 Pro II Frame offers an agile flying experience and exceptional durability, making it the ideal choice for both indoor and outdoor flights.
