## 产品介绍

The Gemfan 1614 3-Blade Propeller (1.0mm Shaft) is an advanced 40mm prop engineered for micro drone performance. Crafted from high-strength PC material, it weighs just 0.24g—making it the lightest 40mm whoop propeller available—and features a 40mm diameter, 1.4‑inch pitch, and 4mm hub thickness. Its low rotational inertia delivers snappy throttle response, smooth handling, and precise control even during demanding flight. Available in Transparent Gray and Transparent, these propellers are the ideal match for 0802 Brushless Motors (2026), perfectly complementing the power and agility of the Air75 II whoop.
