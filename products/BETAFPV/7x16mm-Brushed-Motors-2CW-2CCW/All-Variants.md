# BETAFPV 7x16mm Brushed Motors (2CW+2CCW)

## 产品介绍

Note: 7x16mm 17500KV brushed motors are discontinued. You can choose its great alternative 19000KV brushed motors.

The Beta65S frame V4 & V3 fit 7x16mm 19000KV Brushed Motors.

### BULLET POINT

- The most powerful 7x16mm motors are upgraded from 17500KV to 19000KV. We are sure that these motors will turn your micro whoop into a powerful monster.

- Weight is 2.95g per motor (including cables and connector)

- With JST 1.25mm 2P connector, perfect for Blade Inductrix, Whoop Drone flight controller

- These 7x16mm motors are only fit for BETAFPV Beta65S frame and Eachine E011 frame for the moment. It does not fit the regular Whoop frames
