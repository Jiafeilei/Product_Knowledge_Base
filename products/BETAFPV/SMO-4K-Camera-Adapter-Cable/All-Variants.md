# BETAFPV SMO 4K Camera Adapter Cable

## 产品介绍

SMO 4K Camera Adapter Cable is designed for those pilots who already own Beta85X V2, Beta95X V2 and Beta95X V2 HD Digital VTX. With this adapter cable, there is no need to disassemble the drone and replace the cable. Just plug to quickly convert the connecter to power the SMO 4K camera like Beta95X V3 Whoop Quadcopter and Beta95X V3 HD Digital VTX.

### Specification

- Item: SMO 4K Camera Adapter Cable

- Input Connector: GH1.25 3-Pin

- Output Connector: SH1.0 4-Pin

- Weight: 0.7g/pc

- Designed for Beta95X V2 and Beta85X V2 to install SMO 4K camera

### Package

- 2 * SMO 4K Camera Adapter Cable
