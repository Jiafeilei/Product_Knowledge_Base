# BETAFPV Snow 2306 Brushless Motors

## 产品介绍

BETAFPV Snow 2306 Brushless Motors are made of top materials and come in a new design with a snowflake appearance style. It features a high-strength TC4 Titanium alloy shaft, N52 magnet, NMB bearing, and 7075 Aluminum rotator for great performance and durable use, working together to make our Snow 2306 one of the most powerful FPV brushless motors out there. Perfectly suited for FPV racing and FPV freestyle on 5"-6" quadcopters.

### Bullet Point

- Made of top materials. High-strength Titanium TC4 shaft, N52 magnet, NMB bearing, and 7075 Aviation Aluminum Alloy are applied to Snow 2306 motors, making the motors durable for use.

- With a snowflake appearance style design. The whole motors show a silver white color in the air, looking very nice.

- Compatible with 5"-6" quadcopters for FPV racing and FPV freestyle. It has 1860KV and 2500KV two motor KV versions for choice.

-
Snow 2306 Brushless Motors and 5" props GF Freestyle 3S 3-Blade Props, GF 51433 3-Blade Props, GF 51466 MCK V2 3-Blade Props match perfectly, which will give you a super-efficient flight experience.

### Specification

- Item: Snow 2306 Brushless Motors

- Dimensions: φ29.3*30.6mm

- Shaft Length: 12.3mm

- Shaft Diameter: 5mm

- Motor KV (rpm/V): 1860KV/2500KV (optional)

- Configuration: 12N14P

- Input Voltage: 4S/6S Lipo

- Wire Length: 20AWG 160mm

- Weight: 34g (with wires)

- Shaft Style: TC4 Titanium Shaft

- Iron Core: 0.15mm Silicon Steel

- Magnet: Sintered NdFeB N52SH

- End Cap: 7075 Aviation Aluminum Alloy

- Bearing: NMB Bearing
