# BETAFPV 31mm 4-blade Micro Whoop Propellers (1.0mm Shaft)

## 产品介绍

These 31mm props are just customed for 65mm brushless drones that has 1.0mm motor shaft. The compatiable motors include Sunnysky 0703 motors, Racerstar 0703 motors, and Boldclash 0703 motors.

### Specification

- Product material: Clear Plastic

- Inner diameter:  1.0mm Shaft

- Height: 7.2mm

- Weight (2CW+2CCW): 1.20(g) = 0.04(oz)

### Recommended Parts

- Drone: Beta65X / Beta65 Pro 2 / Beta65 Pro

- Frame: Beta65 Pro 1S brushless frame or Beta65X 2S Whoop frame

- Motor: 0603 brushless motors / 0703 motors of Sunsky, Racerstar and Boldclash

### Package

- 2 * CW 4-blade 31mm Whoop Props (1.0mm shaft)

- 2 * CCW 4-blade 31mm Whoop Props (1.0mm shaft)
