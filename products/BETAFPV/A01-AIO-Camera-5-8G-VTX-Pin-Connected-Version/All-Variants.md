# BETAFPV A01 AIO Camera 5.8G VTX (Pin-Connected Version)

## 产品介绍

A01 AIO Camera 5.8G VTX is upgraded.

- Add JST0.8 connector on A01 VTX, plug-and-play design makes it easy to install to the camera without soldering.

- Replaced the welded antenna with an IPX type of antenna, just plug and play, no extra solder work needed.

The A01 AIO Camera combo is perfect for Pro2 Series whoop drone, which is integrated with A01 VTX, 5.8GHz FPV Transmitter, C02 Camera and Dipole Whip Antenna. It’s compatible with 5.8G FPV Goggle VR006.

### Bullet Point

- Micro-sized, lightweight

- Integrated Camera and 0mW/25mW/200mW adjustable transmitter

- Support PitMode, power up VTX during race events without interfering

- Dipole whip high sensitivity antenna. It is mounted to the VTX board durable.

- Included with SmartAudio function. FC can communicate with the VTX by using the audio line-in, which allows FC to know what frequency the VTX is using, and to change it

- 30° tilt Camera Mounting hardware is provided to suit your preference
