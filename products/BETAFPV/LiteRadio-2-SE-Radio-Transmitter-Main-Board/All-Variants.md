# BETAFPV LiteRadio 2 SE Radio Transmitter Main Board

## 产品介绍

This is the main board of the LiteRadio 2 SE radio transmitter.  It is compatible with the remote controller case of all LiteRadio 2 and LiteRadio 2 SE. For the owner of this radio transmitter, geting this main board is the most budget way to try the most popular ExpressLRS 2.4G radio link protocol.

### SPECIFICATIONS

- 8 channes in total

- Protocol ExpressLRS 2.4G

- RF power support 25mW, 50mW and 100mW

- Packet rate support 50Hz, 150Hz, 250Hz and 500Hz

- BETAFPV Configurator supported for updating and configuring

- USB joystick support for most practice simulator

- 1S battery supported and built-in charging circuit
