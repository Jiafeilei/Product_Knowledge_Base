# BETAFPV Meteor75 Pro II FPV Kit

## 产品介绍

The Meteor75 Pro II FPV Kit brings together the new Meteor75 Pro II whoop, VR03 goggles, LiteRadio 2 SE transmitter with a portable carry bag – everything you need to dive into immersive FPV flying right out of the box. As the more advanced 1S analog ready‑to‑fly package, this kit delivers exceptional performance and durability, giving you the confidence to explore both indoor and outdoor environments with ease. Whether you’re a novice or a seasoned pilot, the Meteor75 Pro II FPV Kit is your perfect gateway to the thrill of FPV.

### Bullet Points

- Upgraded 1S Whoop: 80mm wheelbase, Freestyle 1102 motors, GF 1811 3-B Props and LAVA II 1S 580mAh battery deliver excellent 6.33:1 thrust‑to‑weight ratio and 10.5 minutes indoor/outdoor flight.

- All‑in‑One Ready‑to‑Fly Kit: Includes Meteor75 Pro II drone, VR03 goggles, LiteRadio 2 SE radio transmitter, and a portable carry bag.

- VR03 Goggles: Feature a replaceable high‑quality 5.8G 3dBi omnidirectional antenna and a 4.3‑inch 800×480 high‑brightness LCD for clear, stable video recording during flight.

- LiteRadio 2 SE: Ergonomic game‑controller design with a built‑in 1000mAh battery delivering up to 8 hours of use on a single charge.

- Built for Durability & Portability: Rugged whoop frame and included carry bag make it easy to take anywhere and survive inevitable crashes.

### Complete Kit, Portable & Ready to Fly

This all-in-one kit includes the robust Meteor75 Pro II whoop, VR03 goggles, and LiteRadio 2 SE radio transmitter—everything you need to start flying FPV immediately. Additionally, a custom portable bag is included for safe transport to the field, races, or vacations. Simply unbox and take to the skies in minutes.

### Superior Performance & Durability

Featuring the all-new optimized II frame with aerodynamic refinements and tough construction, the Meteor75 Pro II delivers exceptional crash resistance and flight efficiency. The advanced Matrix 1S 5IN1 II FC provides reliable power delivery and crash-proof durability, while the ultra-wide 173° FOV Air Camera in 16:9 aspect ratio ensures immersive, crystal-clear FPV vision for confident flying. Whether you're a beginner learning the basics or an experienced pilot pushing freestyle limits, the Meteor75 Pro II is built to perform—and built to last.

### Powerful Propulsion System

Powered by the Freestyle 1102 Motors (2026), featuring an upgraded 2000 series alloy outer rotor housing material and a reinforced shaft design built to withstand the hardest impacts. Achieving 15% higher efficiency through improved slot fill factor, these motors deliver more power output and smoother throttle response when paired with GF 1811 3-Blade Props. With the LAVA II 1S 580mAh batteries, the Meteor75 Pro II achieves up to 10.5 minutes of flight time and a 6.33:1 thrust-to-weight ratio—giving you extended sessions to capture the perfect shot.

### VR03 FPV Goggles

The VR03 goggles come with a replaceable high‑quality 5.8G 3dBi omnidirectional antenna that receives signals equally from all horizontal directions, ensuring stable video transmission and vivid images during flight. A 4.3‑inch 800×480 high‑brightness LCD delivers a bright, warp‑free image specifically tuned for FPV racing. Record your flights directly to a microSD card and replay footage right on the goggles. The classic, ergonomic design features a foam sponge faceplate and a three‑sided adjustable headband for a secure, comfortable fit. At just 300g, these compact goggles are lightweight and easy to carry wherever you fly.

### LiteRadio 2 SE Radio Transmitter

The LiteRadio 2 SE features an ergonomic, game‑controller shape that fits comfortably in your hands. Powered by a built‑in 1000mAh 1S battery, it delivers up to 8 hours of continuous use for excellent endurance. With 100mW transmitting power, it offers a wide operating range for reliable control. The transmitter supports popular FPV simulators including VelociDrone, Liftoff, TRYP, DRL, DCL, and DJI Virtual Flight, and is compatible with desktops, Android devices, and tablets – making it easy to practice and sharpen your skills anytime, anywhere.

### HD Digital VTX vs. Analog VTX

The Meteor75 Pro HD kit is equipped with the P1 Air Unit HD VTX and a digital‑ready Matrix 3IN1 FC, paired with the VR04 HD goggles and LiteRadio 4 SE for a smooth HD flight experience. The Meteor75 Pro II analog kit comes with the Matrix 5IN1 II FC and analog VTX, paired with the VR03 goggles and LiteRadio 2 SE. Choose the video system that suits you best.
