# BETAFPV 4S Whoop Cable Pigtail (XT30)

## 产品介绍

This power cable with XT30 connector is specially made for 4S whoop drone, soldering with the capacitor specification with 25V/220μF/20%, for protecting ESC board, reducing the defective rate caused by the instantaneous high voltage, Its supportive power is up to 4S, fits for F4 V2 4S FC and F4 2-4S AIO FC on Beta85X 4S.

### Bullet Point

- This XT30 cable has added the capacitor specification with 25V/220μF/20%, for protecting ESC board. The temperature resistance is -20°C~120°C.

- The whoop cable pigtail with XT30 connector can support 2-4S power, you can use F4 V2 4S FC and 4S batteries on Beta85X, get more punch on power.

- Weighs only 3.3g/pc. The material of power cable is 18AWG silicone, the internal material of 18AWG Silicone Wires are Nylon/PA.

- High quality and super flexible. Firm plug connector on the Amass XT30, no need to worry loose easily, the cable length is 65mm including the plug.

### Specification

- Power: 2-4S

- Weight: 3.2g/pc

- Connector: XT30

- Internal Material: Nylon/PA

- Body Material: Gold-plated copper

- Length: 65mm(Including the plug)

- Temperature resistance: -20°C~120°C

- Capacitor specification: 25V/220μF/20%

- Wire: 18AWG Heatproof Soft Silicone Cable

- Adapt FC: F4 V2 4S FC,  F4 2-4S AIO FC

- Adapt Drone: Beta85X 4S, Beta85X 4S HD, Beta85X 4K Whoop Drone

### Package

- 6 * 4S Whoop Power Cable(XT30)
