# BETAFPV HX100 SE T300 Carbon Fiber Frame

## 产品介绍

This HX100 SE T300 Carbon Fiber Frame is customized for HX100 SE FPV Quad.

### Bullet Point

- Made of T300 carbon fiber material, ultra-light body makes the drone fast and steady in flight.

- Suitable for BETAFPV F4 1S brushless flight controller V2.1 and 1102 13500KV motor. Make your 1S toothpick whoop drone.

- Only weighs 4.0g/pc with 2mm thickness, wheelbase is 100mm.

- The parcel comes with plastic durable package in default, which can protect the package from deformation during the shipping process.

### Specification

- Material: T300 Carbon Fiber

- Diagonal motor distance: 100mm

- Motor mounting hole: for 1102 13500 motors (Compatible with 1103 motors)

- FC mounting hole: fully compatible with whoop style FC board mounting pattern, 26mm x 26mm

- Weight: 4.0g/pc

### Recommended Parts

- Propellers: Gemfan 65mm 2-blade props(1.5mm shaft hole)

- Motors: 1102 13500KV Brushless Motors

- Batteries: BT2.0 300mAh 1S Battery

- FC & ESC: F4 1S Brushless FC

- Drone: HX100 SE FPV Quad

### Package

- 1 * HX100 SE T300 Carbon Fiber Frame

- 1 * 85mm Battery Cable Tie

- 1 * No-Slip Rubber Pads

- 1 * Pack of Screws
