### EREADRONE: THE FPV SIMULATOR

EreaDrone is the most physics realistic drone racing simulator. Thanks to this simulator, you will be able to train and fly with a BetaFPV drone! There are 7 freestyle maps including 2 dedicated ones for the BetaFPV drones.

Because of a partnership with the ENSMA (Ecole Nationale Supérieure de Mécanique et d’Aérotechnique), the physicsbehaviors of the drone will be so close you can barely see the difference.

In EreaDrone, few drones are available: a 5-inch drone racing, the Beta75X, and maybe soon, the Beta85X.

### HOW TO INSTALL IT?

All you will need is to have a Steam account. Steam is the biggest gaming platform in the world. Once you have downloaded the Steam software you will be able to get EreaDrone.

If you have a Steam Key (in the BetaFPV drone kit with EreaDrone), you can activate it through the button on the left in Steam: “Add a Game”, then “Activate a Product on Steam”. Now, you can enter the Steam Key and the installation of EreaDrone starts directly.

Quickly Enter the steam here: http://www.ereadrone.com

Nearly all controllers are compatibles with EreaDrone Simulator. If you are using a real RC controller, check if you can plug it into your computer with a USB cable like the FrSky Taranis or with an adaptor available in many RC stores.
