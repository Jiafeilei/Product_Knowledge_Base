# BETAFPV TWIG XL 3" Frame Kit

## 产品介绍

TWIG XL is a larger version of our original TWIG frame. All the XL frames are premium 3mm thick, high grade T700 carbon cut along the weave and chamfered on both sides interior and exterior edges at .7mm. All the frames are approximately 140mm motor post to motor post and swing up to 3'' props. The frames accommodate motor mounting for 4-hole 9x9 or 12x12 motor mounts. TWIG XL 3'' FPV Toothpick Quad is built basced this frame and with the Caddx Ratel Camera, 1404 3800KV Brushless Motors, 12A Toothpick F4 2-4S AIO FC.

See more about the TWIG XL at Racer X FPV!
Check out the Twig Builders Group on FB!

### Bullet point

- High-grade 3mm thick T700 carbon cut along the weave and chamfered on both sides interior and exterior edges at .7mm

- 20x20 or whoop/toothpick stack mounting

- Slotted motor mounts for 9x9 to 12x12mm motors

- 5-55 degree camera angle adjustment
