# BETAFPV Pagoda 2 5.8G  FPV Antenna (2PCS)

## 产品介绍

Note: There are SMA Male and RP-SMA Male for you to choose.

This thoughtfully designed circular polarized antenna utilizes a 3 tiered 1 oz copper PCBs to replace the typically fragile wire elements.
It will work great as either TX or RX antenna and for best performance we recommend using it as matched set. They will be sold in a set of 2 antennas with the option for Stubby at 50mm and Standard 85mm lengths.

### ﻿BULLET POINT

- This Antenna comes with case and only weighs 9 gm, it will include the optional heavy duty polyolefin heat shrink to help protect PCB from damage.

- It has a very good axial ratio measurement <1.3 and SWR of 1.2.

- Omni-directional, no dead corner.High gain, more stable. Less flash, stonger siginal

- Update version. Better then most other antenna.
