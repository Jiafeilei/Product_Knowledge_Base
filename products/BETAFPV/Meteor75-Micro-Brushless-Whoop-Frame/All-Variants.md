# BETAFPV Meteor75 Micro Brushless Whoop Frame

## 产品介绍

Brand new lightweight 75mm 1S brushless frame for Meteor75 Brushless Whoop Quadcopter is released now! This frame is ultralight only 5.68g, but it still durable enough. Specially designed the following point.

- Adjusted the height of the frame, the lowest point of frame is the motor position, battery can be protected while the drone is landing.

- Increased the height of the frame loop to protect the propeller better.

Best recommended for Advanced kit drone - Meteor75 Lite and Meteor75.

###
Bullet Point

- Meteor75 frame is ultralight only 5.68g, light but durable enough.

- The motor mount position uses a quadrilateral stretch structure, the frame is more durable and stable.

- The height of the frame has been adjusted, the lowest point of the frame is the motor position, the battery can be protected while the drone is landing. It also increased the height of the frame loop to protect the propeller better.

- Designed two different motor mounting holes that support 0603, 0802 and 1102 brushless motors at the same time.

- With a 26mm*26mm FC mounting columns can match whoop style FC.

### Specification

- Item: Meteor75 frame

- Material: Plastic

- Size: 75mm

- Weight: 5.68/pc

- Color: White/Blue

- Motor mounting hole: For 0603 motor, 1102 Series Motors and 0802 Series Motors

- FC mounting hole: 26mm x 26mm, fully compatible with whoop style FC board mounting pattern

### Recommended Parts

- Propellers: 40mm 4-blade props (1.5mm shaft hole)

- Motors: 0603 and 0802 Series Brushless Motors /1102 Series Motors

- Batteries: BT2.0 450mAh 1S Battery, BT2.0 550mAh 1S Battery

- Screws: Meteor Series Motor Fixing Screws Pack (40PCS)

- FC: F4 AIO 1S Brushless FC/ 1-2S AIO Brushless FC

- 1 * Meteor75 Micro Brushless Whoop Frame
