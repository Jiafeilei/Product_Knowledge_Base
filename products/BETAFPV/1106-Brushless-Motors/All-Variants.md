Rated at 3800KV / 4500KV, 1106 Brushless Motor hits the sweet spot for high-powered 4s power. This motor has been designed and built for your 4S 85-120mm racing or freestyle drone, resulting in incredible power and performance.

- Customized for 4S 85-120mm drones self-assembly, which is compatible for HX100, Beta85X drones, Beta95X Frame Kit and Beta95X V3 Frame Kit.

- Factory Balanced Precision CNC Motor Bells.

- Extensive and rough testing ensures its ability to withstand some of the worst crashes, offers incredible power, efficiency, and torque.
