# BETAFPV GemFan D90S 3-Blade Propellers (1.5mm Shaft)

## 产品介绍

For those seeking unparalleled thrust during cinematic FPV maneuvers, the GemFan D90S 3-Blade Propellers provide an exceptional solution. With a 3-blade design, these propellers enhance airborne grip and maximize surface area, resulting in heightened thrust efficiency.  Weighing a mere 2.6g, these lightweight propellers possess a minimal moment of inertia, facilitating rapid RPM adjustments and elevating drone responsiveness. When combined with the LAVA Series 2006 Brushless Motors, these propellers exhibit exceptional response speed, empowering pilots to capture breathtaking footage seamlessly.
