# BETAFPV P1 HD VRX (Nano)

We sincerely thank all pilots for their invaluable support and feedback during the Beta Test phase. The final product is now here (also available — the Matrix 1S AIO P1 HD FC), featuring the following upgrades:

- Power Supply – Now supports power supply directly from your goggles.

- Optimized HDMI Cable – Upgraded from a straight connection to a 90° angled cable for a clean look.

The P1 HD VRX (Nano) is a compact digital video receiver compatible with the ArtLynk Protocol, designed to bring crystal‑clear HD FPV to your existing Fat Shark and SKYZONE goggles without adding unnecessary bulk. Simply connect it via the mini HDMI port to Fat Shark or SKYZONE goggles and enjoy a stunning 1080p@60fps live feed with low latency and reliable 400m+ range. With multi‑band switching for interference‑free video and a plug‑and‑play design, the P1 HD VRX Nano is the most affordable way to upgrade your FPV experience to digital HD.

### Compact & Lightweight

Designed to be unobtrusive on your existing goggles, the P1 HD VRX (Nano) is small, lightweight, and perfectly balanced for comfortable, long‑duration flying. No extra weight. No bulky hardware. Just crisp, clear HD video – fly as long as you like.

Note: For a cleaner, more streamlined installation, we upgraded the standard straight cable to a 90° angled HDMI cable – now available in both Fat Shark and SKYZONE versions.

### Fat Shark & SKYZONE Compatible — Bring Your Classic Goggles Back to Life

Available in two case sizes, the P1 HD VRX (Nano) is compatible with most Fat Shark (39×62×36mm) and SKYZONE (32×64×36mm) FPV goggles. Simply connect via the mini HDMI port to upgrade your existing goggles to a digital receiver – no need to invest in expensive digital goggles. It's the most affordable way to experience HD FPV with your current setup.

Note: The previous incompatibility with Fat Shark Dominator HD3 and V3 has been resolved via a firmware update for the P1 HD VRX Nano. To work with these goggles, manually switch the resolution to 720P. Simply press and hold the 5D and Back buttons simultaneously for 5 seconds. Wait for the device to reboot – the resolution will switch automatically.

The P1 HD VRX supports 1080p@60fps video transmission with a stable 400m+ range and low latency for a smooth, immersive flying experience. ### Know More About ArtLynk Protocol

The ArtLynk protocol is a low-latency, long-range communication protocol for drones, offering superior stability and range over traditional analog VTX. ArtLynk protocol enables real-time HD video at about 60ms latency from glass to glass, which is crucial for smooth flight control and high-quality FPV (First Person View). Here are the key features you may want to know.

- Artosyn Inside & KAP Supported: ArtLynk protocol uses the Artosyn's latest AR803X chipset, with core technology support provided by KAP (KAP company provides core technology support or product solution to all FPV companies and ensures compatibility for various FPV products using Artosyn chipsets).

-
Collaborative and Open Community: ArtLynk protocol is a collaborative FPV ecosystem not belonging to ONLY one company. Many FPV enterprises and teams would design and produce their own products based on the ArtLynk protocol, ensuring broad compatibility and fostering community.

The ArtLynk protocol supports the following devices, letting you customize your own build or simply bind and fly:

- BETAFPV P1 Air Unit, VR04 HD Goggles, Aquila20 P1, Meteor75 Pro P1, Monitor, P1 HD VRX (Standard), P1 HD VRX (Nano), Matrix 1S AIO P1 HD FC

- HGLRC Draco

- TJRC VTX, XJRC XJ3D Goggles, XJRC 02L Goggles

- More coming soon!
