# BETAFPV Canopy II for O4 Air Unit

## 产品介绍

The Canopy II for O4 Air Unit boasts a redesigned vibration-damping and camera mounting system, purpose-built for the DJI O4 and O4 Wide Air Units to deliver superior stability, complete protection, and effortless installation. A secure four-screw fixation system, combined with flexible mounting holes, offers 15° to 35° camera angle adjustments for versatile shooting across any scenario.

### Bullet Points

- Enhanced Vibration Damping: Four shock-absorbing balls effectively eliminate jello for professional-grade footage.

- Simplified Installation: Quick and easy to install and remove versus first-generation design.

- Complete Camera Protection: New form factor ensures full compatibility and robust protection for both DJI O4 and O4 Wide Air Units.

- Adjustable Tilt Range: 15° to 35° angle adjustments for versatile shooting.

- DIY-Friendly: Standard 25.5mm x 25.5mm mounting holes for customization flexibility.

### More Stability, Even Simpler

Featuring a four shock-absorbing ball connection system with the Meteor II Frame, the Canopy II for O4 Air Unit delivers outstanding vibration damping for smooth, crystal-clear HD footage. Compared to the first generation's complicated installation, the Canopy II draws on the proven O4 Pro bracket solution from the Pavo series, making it significantly easier to install and remove while delivering enhanced stability throughout every flight session. The redesigned form factor ensures complete protection for both DJI O4 and O4 Wide Air Units, safeguarding your investment during aggressive maneuvers.

### Flexible and Stable Camera Design

With a secure four-screw fixation system, the camera remains firmly in place during even the most aggressive maneuvers. Flexible installation holes enable 15° to 35° camera angle adjustments, ensuring flawless HD footage across diverse shooting scenarios.

### New Parts

DJI O4 Camera Mount: This is the dedicated camera mount for the DJI O4 Air Unit. When upgrading to the Canopy II for O4 Air Unit, this mount ensures a secure and precise fit for stable, vibration-free footage.

Pavo20 Pro II and Pavo Pico II Top Plate: For pilots having the Pavo20 Pro II or Pavo Pico II whoops who wish to use the Canopy II, these top plates are the solution. Compatible with the O4 Air Unit Pro Bracket, O4 Air Unit Bracket II, and Canopy II for O4 Air Unit, they offer a versatile new mounting option. See the images below for installation reference.
