# BETAFPV HQ 4025 2-Blade Propellers 1.5mm Shaft

## 产品介绍

These 4025 props are perfect for your 4" toothpick build - incredibly lightweight without sacrificing durability.

4025 props retain φ1.5mm shaft design, which means it needs M2 screws to lock props, while most of 4inch props are locked with nuts in the market. These props are best paired with 1505 Motors and 1404 Motors.

### Project #MicroLR Build

Dave C FPV has released project Micro Long Range-  Scale down a 7" long range quad until it is sub 250g AUW. He makes a video present his concept, which allows you to easily fit an impressive amount of feature in your sub 250 setups. As far as Dave's presentation, Betafpv 1404/1505 motor, and 4025 props will be the best power system worth to build this fantastic drone. Learn more about at Thingiverse article.
