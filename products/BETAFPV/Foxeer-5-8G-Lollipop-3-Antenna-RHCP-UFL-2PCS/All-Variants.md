# BETAFPV Foxeer 5.8G Lollipop 3 Antenna RHCP UFL (2PCS)

## 产品介绍

With the new unibody structure and strength alloy copper material, gives the antenna stronger signal. Easy to manufacture within fine tolerances, even for a hobbyist.

### ﻿BULLET POINT

- The shell of antenna is made of  imported PC and ABS, which can withstand high strength impact, help to protect the antenna from damage.

- Double-jacket semi-rigid cable for heavy impact resistance.

- 360°protection with the IRoot reinforcement design.

- Light weight and tiny design, only 3.6g for 1pcs, convenient for intallation on most drone on the current market. It will work great with 85X and 75X by doing some handwork.

### SPECIFICATION

- Shell Material: Imported PC + ABS

- Connector : UFL

- Polarizabilty: RHCP

- Gain : 2.5Dbi

- Color : Black

- Weight : 3.6g (1 pcs)

- Dimensions : 85.5*15*85.5mm

- Center Frequency : 5750 MHz (5.8GHz)

- Bandwidth: 500 MHz (5.5 – 6.0GHz)

- Axial ratio: <3db

- Matching: VSWR <1.2 (at center frequency)

- Radiation efficiency: 98%

### How to Fit Beta85X / Beta75X

If you want to install the antenna on the Beta85X or Beta75X, please follow the following method.
Step 1: Cut off the extra antenna, leaves about 25-30mm.

Step 2: Add welding flux on the antenna to make it more easier to solder.

Step 3: Solder the antenna on the vtx board carefully.

Step 4: Attach the cable tie to fix the antenna .
