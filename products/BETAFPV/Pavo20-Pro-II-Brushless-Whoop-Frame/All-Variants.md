# BETAFPV Pavo20 Pro II Brushless Whoop Frame

## 产品介绍

The Pavo20 Pro II Brushless Whoop Frame is tailored for the 2.2″ Pavo20 Pro II cinewhoop, now offering expanded compatibility with both DJI O4 Air Unit, O4 Wide Air Unit and O4 Air Unit Pro through its new carbon plate. Its revolutionary assembly system upgrades to a 4-screw configuration with embedded nuts and supported structure, enabling quicker, more secure builds with exceptional rigidity. Designed with a 93.9mm wheelbase and constructed from crash-proof PA12 thickened whoop ducts, this frame combines durability with aerodynamic efficiency, delivering an 8-11% thrust boost. Perfectly paired with a robust 3S and 4S propulsion systems, it generates unparalleled power for freestyle and cinematic flights. New parts include buzzer for easy recovery, GPS brackets compatible with most 12x and 18x GPS modules.

### Bullet Points

- Hassle-Free Assembly: An integrated injection-molded bracket and supported structure enable a quick, tool-light build secured with just 4 screws—eliminating tedious adjustments.

- O4, O4 Wide & O4 Pro Compatibility: A redesigned carbon plate seamlessly integrates O4, O4 Wide and O4 Pro for stunning HD footage and build versatility.

- Durable PA12 Whoop Duct: Featuring a crash-proof construction with a reinforced cross-section design for superior impact resistance and longevity.

- Aerodynamic Thrust Boost: Engineered airflow dynamics deliver an 8–11% thrust increase compared to ductless designs.

- O4 Air Unit Pro Bracket: Camera protection panel, dual fixing holes and 30° angled antenna mounts gurantee smooth cinematic shooting.

- Pavo Series Rubber Damper Design: Proprietary vibration-damping system guarantees buttery-smooth, jitter-free HD footage in all flight modes.

- Versatile Battery Mounting System: Open-ended strap + side-limitation slot ensures a rock-solid battery grip, accommodating a wide range of battery sizes while preventing shifts during hard maneuvers.

- Buzzer & GPS Brackets: Buzzer for quick crash recovery and two GPS brackets compatible with most 12x and 18x modules ensure flight confidence.

### Compatibility with Popular HD VTX

With a robust 93.9mm wheelbase and crash-proof PA12 thickened whoop ducts, the Pavo20 Pro II Frame is engineered for resilience and agility. Its revolutionary assembly system features embedded nuts and requires only four screws for a secure, crash-resistant lock—making building faster and more reliable than ever. Advanced injection molding combines a supported structure with three guided mounting points, guaranteeing perfect carbon plate alignment and eliminating flex for an incredibly rigid, stable platform.

The newly designed carbon plate expands compatibility to seamlessly support the DJI O4 Air Unit Pro, O4 Wide Air Unit and O4 Air Unit, future-proofing your setup for stunning HD footage. With pre-tuned PID parameters optimized for both O4 systems, achieving peak performance is effortless—simply select your configuration via the OSD and fly with precision and confidence.

### O4 Air Unit Pro Bracket

The Pavo20 Pro II integrates the advanced O4 Air Unit Pro Bracket, engineered to deliver exceptionally stable, smooth HD footage while streamlining your building experience. It features a reinforced camera side panel for maximum protection and dual fixing holes that optimize the camera's center of rotation, enabling smooth 0-40° tilt adjustments and providing unobstructed ND filter access even in wide-angle scenarios. The 30° angled antenna mounts ensure optimal signal strength, while tool-free installation allows for quick antenna swaps. Securely assembled with just 8 half-thread screws, the bracket eliminates tedious assembly while enhancing anti-vibration performance.

### Buzzer & GPS Brackets

The buzzer makes crash recovery quick and effortless—no more searching through tall grass or losing your whoop in dense terrain. For pilots seeking advanced navigation and return-to-home functionality, two GPS brackets can be compatible with most 12x and 18x GPS modules on the market. Whether you're exploring new spots or pushing your limits, these features provide the peace of mind to fly with confidence.
