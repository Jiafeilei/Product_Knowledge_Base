# BETAFPV TBS Crossfire Nano Receiver

## 产品介绍

TBS is stepping up the Long Range / UHF game, with no competition in sight! With the TBS Crossfire Nano RX we condense 2 years of engineering experience, listening to our customer requirements, and pushing ourselves to the maximum (or in this case, the minimum!). The end result is a full range receiver, at the smallest size of any receiver in the industry.

TBS Crossfire Nano RX is for people who want to save space and solder. Full range, designed specifically for mini- and micro-quads!

- Please use with Crossfire firmware V6.10 or above.

### Specifications

- Signal format: CRSF

- Frequency min: 868 MHz

- Frequency max: 915 MHz

- Input voltage Min: 3.3 V

- Input voltage Max: 8.4 V

- Range: 40 km

- Channels: 12

- Output formats: PPM/SBUS/CRSF/PWM

- Weight: 0.5 g

- Height: 11 mm

- Width: 18 mm

- Length: 11 mm

### Package

- 1 * TBS Crossfire Nano Receiver

- 1 * TBS Crossfire Micro Receiver Antenna
