# BETAFPV BETAFPV Waterslide Decals

## 产品介绍

Looking for a way to make your drone and gears your uniquely? BETAFPV Waterslide Decals and Model Decal are exquisite offerings tailored specifically for enhancing the exteriors of model drones. It presents a diverse array of captivating patterns, empowering players to personalize their models and attain an impressively stylish aesthetic.

### Bullet Points

- High-precision screen printing.

- The water decal adopts a narrow edge design and fits the model more naturally.

- Use tension transfer film, and stick on the curved parts without breaking and wrinkling.

- Under UV light, the BETAFPV Waterslide Decals will have a unique fluorescent effect.

### Waterslide Decals

Enhance your quadcopter's aesthetic with these incredible decals! What makes these so special is that they react under ultraviolet (UV) light. Prepare to be amazed by their stunning appearance even in their original form!

### Model Decals

The patterns are meticulously crafted using cutting-edge industrial printing techniques, ensuring impeccable clarity of fonts and steadfast adhesion. These decals not only provide discreet safeguarding against scratches but also do so without adding any bulk. With their effortless, swift, and precise application, they effortlessly adhere to the surface, leaving no residue upon removal.

### Instruction for Use

- Cut the decal from the rest of the decals sheet with your hobby knife.

- Soak the decal in water for about 10 seconds until the decal can perfectly slide off the paper.

- Use tweezers and your hobby knife to get the decal onto the model.

- Dab the decal with a paper towel to soak up extra water.
