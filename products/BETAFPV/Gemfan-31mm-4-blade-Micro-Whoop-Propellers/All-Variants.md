# BETAFPV Gemfan 31mm 4-blade Micro Whoop Propellers

## 产品介绍

These 31mm props are just customized for 65mm brushless drones that have a 1.0mm or 0.8mm motor shaft. The recommended motors for 1.0mm are 0603 motor, 0703 motor and 080X motor like 0802 motor. Besides, here are the 31mm 3-blade props (1.0mm Shaft) and 31mm 3-blade props (0.8mm Shaft) for your choice.

### Specification

- Item: 31mm 4-blade Props

- Material: PC

- Center Thickness: 5mm

- Prop Disk Diameter: 31mm

- Center Hole Inner diameter: 0.8mm / 1.0mm

- Prop Width: 5.71mm

- Weight: 0.31g

-
Colors: Blue / White / Grey

### Recommended Parts

Gemfan 31mm 4-blade Micro Whoop Propellers (1.0mm Shaft)

- Drone: Beta65X / Beta65 Pro 2 / Beta65 Pro / Meteor65

- Frame: Beta65 Pro frame / Beta65X 2S frame / Meteor65 frame

- Motor: 0603 motors / 0802 motor / 0703 motor

Gemfan 31mm 4-blade Micro Whoop Propellers (0.8mm Shaft)

- Drone: Beta65S Lite Brushed V3

- Frame: Beta65S Lite Brushed frame

- Motor: 7x16mm Brushed Motors
