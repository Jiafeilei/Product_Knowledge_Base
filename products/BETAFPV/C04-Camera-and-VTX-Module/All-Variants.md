# BETAFPV C04 Camera and VTX Module

## 产品介绍

The modular C04 Camera and VTX Module for the Cetus X quadcopter offer easy installation and usage. Simply plug and play. With its lightweight 4.14g design and 1200TVL resolution, this camera delivers outstanding performance for micro FPV quadcopters. It provides a wide 160° field of view and utilizes a 1/3'' CMOS sensor for crisp and vibrant images. By purchasing and replacing it on your Cetus X quadcopter, pilots can secure emergency backup and minimize downtime.

### Bullet Points

- The modular design allows for easy installation and use, without the need for professional soldering skills. Simply plug and play directly.

- The max output power is up to 350~400mW, so pilots can fly longer, and farther explore more possibilities, and have fun.

- Miniature, and lightweight design, weight at only 4.14g with antenna and adjustable output power for longer-distance flying.

- 1200TVL resolution and 1/3" CMOS sensor ensure good quality and a clear image.

- With a wider 160° FOV, it can record wide and beautiful scenes.

- If you have the Betaflight Version flight controller for your Cetus X quadcopter, please select the M04 Version C04 Camera and VTX Module. Otherwise, choose the Cetus V2 Version.

### Specifications

- Item: C04 Camera and VTX Module (M04 VTX/Cetus VTX V2)

- Camera: C04 FPV Micro Camera

- Image Sensor: 1/3" CMOS Sensor

- Horizontal Resolution: 1200TVL

- FOV: 160°

- Video System: NTSC (4:3)

- Output power: 25-400mW (M04 VTX)/ 25-350mW (Cetus VTX V2)

- Supply Voltage: 5V

- Size: 14.5*21*23.5mm (without antenna)

- Weight: 4.14g (with antenna)

- Frequency: 5 bands 40 channels with Raceband (M04 VTX)，1 band 8 channels (B band) (Cetus VTX V2)
