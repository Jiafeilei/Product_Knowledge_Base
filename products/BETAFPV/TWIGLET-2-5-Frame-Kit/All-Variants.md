# BETAFPV TWIGLET 2.5'' Frame Kit

## 产品介绍

Racer X FPV introduces their smallest micros, the TWIGLET and TWIGLET MINI! These are smaller versions of their original TWIG kits. The TWIGLET is a 100mm (motor post to motor post) 1.5mm thick true-x frame that can accommodate up to 65mm props. Cut from a very high quality carbon and chamfered 0.6mm on both sides, it not only looks amazing, but is also far more durable than most others in its class!
These are deluxe kits, featuring 3 sizes of interchangeable locking TPU battery holders. (New design allows standard or "toilet tank" battery mounting)

Notice: TWIGLET 2.5'' Frame has updates and removed the hole in the center plate, brings a more durable structure.
See more about the TWIGLET at Racer X FPV!
Check out the Twig Builders Group on FB!
