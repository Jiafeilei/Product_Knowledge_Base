# BETAFPV Meteor65 Pro Brushless Whoop Frame

## 产品介绍

The latest 66mm 1S brushless frame for Meteor65 Pro Quadcopter. With a marvelous design on battery slot which keeps a low profile and triangle bilateral stretch structure of motor mounting position, this frame weighs 3.20g only.

###
Bullet Points

- Compared to the previous 65mm brushless frames, with the low profile of battery slot height, the drone's gravity center is reduced and improved the sense of compactness.

- The space for the FC board is limited in order to support 35mm propellers on a 66mm drone. So this Meteor65 Pro frame could only support cross shape FC board. The normal whoop FC board DO NOT fit this frame.

- Meteor65 Pro frame’s motor mounting position uses a triangle bilateral stretch structure, making the frame more aesthetic. It provides you more durable and smoother flight experience.

- The ultra-light and durable frame is PP material only 3.20g, which is one of the lightest frames that can match the F4 1S 5A AIO Brushless Flight Controller (ELRS 2.4G).

- The frame is suitable for 31-35mm propeller like Gemfan 35mm-3 propellers. Pay attention, HQ 35mm propellers is a little bit bigger and DO NOT fit for this frame.

### Colored Frame Series

Meteor65 Pro Brushless Whoop Quadcopter has become more popular among most pilots since its release. Not only durable as before and also with more colors. Now, we published new frames with 10 new colors for Meteor65 Pro. Choosing the one color you like, or collecting them all to mix and match the drones.

### Specifications

- Item: Meteor65 Pro Frame

- Material: PP

- Size: 66mm

- FC mounting hole: 26mm x 26mm, compatible with cross shape whoop style FC board mounting pattern. DO NOT compatible with normal whoop FC board.

- Motor mounting hole: For 0802SE 19500KV motor

- Weight: 3.20g

- Suitable propeller: 35mm or 31mm propellers. Recommend Gemfan 35mm-3 propellers. Pay attention, HQ 35mm propellers is a little bit bigger and DO NOT fit for this frame.

### Recommended Parts

Meteor65 Pro frame is only compatible with cross shape FC board and Gemfan 35mm-3 propellers.

- Propellers: Gemfan 35mm 3-Blade Props

- Motors: 0802SE 19500KV motor

- Batteries: BT2.0 300mAh 1S Battery

- FC: F4 1S 5A AIO Brushless Flight Controller (ELRS 2.4G)

- Screws: Meteor Series Motor Fixing Screws Pack (40PCS)

Note: Normal whoop FC board is NOT compatible with this frame.

Note: HQ 35mm propellers is a little bit bigger and DO NOT fit for this frame.
