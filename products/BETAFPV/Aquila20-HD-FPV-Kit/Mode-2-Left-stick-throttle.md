### Immersive Digital HD Experience

Enter a new era of FPV with the Aquila20 HD's revolutionary digital system. Experience the generational leap from analog to crystal-clear digital, where every detail appears in stunning 1080p resolution at 60fps.

ArtLynk System is a low-latency, long-range communication protocol for drones, offering superior stability and range over traditional analog VTX. It enables real-time HD video at about 60ms latency from glass to glass, which is crucial for smooth flight control and high-quality FPV (First Person View). Here are the key features you may want to know.

- Artosyn Inside & KAP Supported: ArtLynk system uses the Artosyn's latest AR803X chipset, with core technology support provided by KAP (KAP company provides core technology support or product solution to all FPV companies and ensures compatibility for various FPV products using Artosyn chipsets).

-
Collaborative and Open Community: ArtLynk system is a collaborative FPV ecosystem not belonging to ONLY one company. Many FPV enterprises and teams would design and produce their own products based on the ArtLynk system, ensuring broad compatibility and fostering community.

Equipped with a 1/2.9” CMOS sensor and a wide 170° field of view,  the camera captures vibrant, detailed footage in varied lighting and supports 1080P recording. This expanded perspective gives you greater situational awareness, making every flight more immersive and responsive. See more of the world below with clarity that matches the high‑definition transmission—so you can fly confidently, spot details effortlessly, and capture stunning aerial views.

### Learn with Confidence, Fly like a Pro

The Aquila20 HD is designed to grow with you. Powered by the self-developed flight controller, it features three distinct flight modes (N/S/M) that allow you to start with absolute stability and gradually unlock full acrobatic freedom as your skills develop.

Altitude Hold & Stable Hover (N Mode): Your confidence-building mode. A highly precise barometer allows the drone to lock its altitude and hover steadily in place automatically. It's the perfect safety net for taking your hands off the remote control, learning orientation, and ensuring a safe landing in emergencies.

Pro Modes (S & M Modes): For when you're ready to advance. S and M Modes disable all assists, giving experienced pilots complete, unfiltered control for flips, rolls, and precision maneuvering.

Turtle Mode: With this mode, if you flip over, a simple command on your remote control instructs the motors to flip the drone back onto its feet, so you can take off again instantly—no walking over to manually retrieve it.

Firmware Compatibility Notes:

- To ensure seamless compatibility with third-party ArtLynk protocol equipments (such as HGLRC Draco), please verify that your BETAFPV devices are updated to firmware version V2.0.5 or later.

- The air unit needs to be updated via the ground station. First make sure the ground station and air unit are on the same firmware version, then proceed to update the air unit. And firmware V2.0.5 is not compatible with any V1 versions (V1.0.36 / V1.0.44).
