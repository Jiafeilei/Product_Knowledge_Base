Get back in the air faster with the HexaCharger 1S Charger. This palm‑sized hub charges six 1S batteries at once, available in 24W and 78W Pro outputs for rapid PD 3.0 charging. ### Charging Current

For optimal battery health and performance, set the charging current according to the recommended values in the chart below. Do not exceed the maximum current for your battery.

### Charging Adapter

### Power Adapter Recommendations

HexaCharger: Use a PD 3.0 adapter with at least 30W output.

HexaCharger Pro: Use a PD 3.0 adapter with 100W output or higher.

- ANKER Charger (100W)

- NOVOO RG120 3-Port 120W GaN Charger

- UGREEN Nexode 100W USB-C GaN 4-Port Charger

- AOHI The Youth PD 100W GaN 3-Port Fast Charger

### Adapter Compatibility & Behavior

- If the adapter cannot supply the requested charging current, the charger will reduce the current per port to stay within the adapter’s maximum power limit.

- If the charger detects that the adapter's output is ≤5W (5V/1A), it will charge batteries sequentially by port number, while keeping the other channels off.

- If charger can only detects the adapter's voltage but not the maximum current, power limit is unknown, *  shown next to voltage. If the input voltage is below 5V, - shown next to voltage. No symbol if the adapter is providing full compatible power.

- If using a non-PD adapter, wait 5 seconds after powering on for the charger to recognize the protocol before starting to charge.
