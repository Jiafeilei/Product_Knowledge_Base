# BETAFPV Gemfan Hurricane 51433 3-Blade Propellers (5.0mm Shaft 4PCS)

## 产品介绍

The Gemfan Hurricane 51433 3-Blade has been designed as a high-speed propeller, providing a better flight experience for freestyle and FPV shots. With a prop diameter of 129mm, 3.5 inch pitch, 5mm shaft hole, and weight of 3.8g, these props are perfect for any 5" quads and are suitable for BETAFPV 2306 Brushless Motors.

### Specification

- Material: PC

- Weight: 3.8g

- Pitch: 3.5 inch

- Prop Dia: 129mm

- Center Thickness: 6mm

- Center Hole Dia: 5mm

- Recommended Motor: 2206-2306, BETAFPV 2306 Brushless Motors

- Color: Transparent, Purple, Yellow

### Package

- 2 * CW Hurricane 51433 3-Blade Props  (5.0mm shaft)

- 2 * CCW Hurricane 51433 3-Blade Props  (5.0mm shaft)
