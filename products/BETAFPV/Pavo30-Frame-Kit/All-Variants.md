# BETAFPV Pavo30 Frame Kit

## 产品介绍

No carbon brace version (only mold injected parts) is just replacement for the drone. Carbon brace is needed to assemble a new drone.

### Bullet Point

- Built-in carbon fiber and reserve a storage room for the antenna, Pavo30 frame has a more concise appearance and more convenient for installation.

- Made of high-quality PA12 material, Pavo30 frame kit has higher abrasion resistance, toughness, and shows excellent impact resistance in drop & impact tests.

- Two purchase options for those customers to meet the different needs for configuration. While the no carbon brace is for replacement if the frame crashed.

- The shock mitigation system with cushion make cameras more stable on the flight, which ensures pilots can capture all the beautiful moments of flight with the stabilized and clear video.
