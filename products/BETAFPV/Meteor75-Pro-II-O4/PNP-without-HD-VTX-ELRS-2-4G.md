# BETAFPV Meteor75 Pro II O4 PNP (without HD VTX) / ELRS 2.4G

## 产品介绍

The Meteor75 Pro II O4 is the larger, more powerful counterpart to the Meteor65 Pro II O4, purpose-built for pilots seeking enhanced stability and aggressive performance with the DJI O4 Wide Air Unit. Featuring the advanced Canopy II with optimized vibration damping, it captures smooth, crystal-clear HD footage with ease. The rugged Meteor75 Pro II frame with optimized aerodynamics, high-torque 1102 Freestyle Motors (2026), and GF 1811 3-Blade Props delivers superior flight performance, exceptional durability, and up to 6 minutes of flight time with LAVA II 1S 680mAh batteries. With its powerful propulsion system and advanced vibration damping, the Meteor75 Pro II O4 is the ultimate choice for pilots seeking professional-grade 1S HD performance.

### DJI O4 Wide Air Unit: Ultra-Wide Vision, Stunning Clarity

At the heart of the Meteor75 Pro II O4 is the latest DJI O4 Wide Air Unit, featuring a remarkable 159° ultra-wide field of view that captures an expansive perspective for immersive FPV footage. When navigating tight indoor spaces, the O4 Wide delivers stunning 4K/60fps video with ultra-low latency transmission of just 20ms and crystal-clear digital clarity—ensuring you never miss a detail.

Note: To guarantee performance, selected O4 Wide units undergo pre-shipment sampling and testing. If you receive an activated unit, rest assured it has passed our quality assurance process.

### Canopy II: Stability Redefined, Installation Simplified

The Meteor75 Pro II O4 features a significant improvement in vibration damping with the new Canopy II, compatible with both the DJI O4 and O4 Wide Air Units. A four shock-absorbing ball connection system between the Canopy II and the Meteor75 Pro II Frame provides enhanced stability throughout every flight session while making installation and removal significantly easier. The redesigned form factor ensures complete protection for both O4 configurations, while a secure four-screw fixation system—combined with flexible mounting holes—offers smooth 15° to 35° camera angle adjustments for versatile shooting across any scenario.

###
Meteor75 Pro II Frame: Built for Performance and Durability

Featuring optimized aerodynamics with arm braces refined from 6 to 5 and a curved whoop duct design, the Meteor75 Pro II frame reduces weight and air resistance for improved flight efficiency. Weighing only 5.80g—nearly 2g lighter than the previous version—and built tough to withstand crashes, the frame offers an agile flying experience and exceptional durability, making Meteor75 Pro II O4 the ideal choice for both indoor and outdoor flights.

### Enhanced Propulsion: Power and Efficiency

Powering this enhanced platform is the 1102 Freestyle Motors (2026), featuring an upgraded 2000 series outer rotor housing material and a reinforced shaft design built to withstand the hardest impacts. Achieving improved efficiency through enhanced design, these motors deliver more power output and smoother throttle response when paired with GF 1811 3-Blade Props. With the LAVA II 1S 680mAh batteries, the Meteor75 Pro II O4 enables up to 6 minutes of flight time—giving you extended sessions to capture the perfect shot.

### Matrix 1S 3IN1 HD FC

The Meteor75 Pro II O4 is powered by the cutting-edge Matrix 1S 3IN1 HD FC, purpose-built for 1S HD whoops. Designed exclusively for digital FPV, this FC combines a flight controller, ESC, and ELRS 2.4G RX into a compact, lightweight unit, eliminating analog OSD for seamless performance. With a newly developed BEC and ESC, the Matrix 1S 3IN1 HD FC ensures stable digital image transmission, even in challenging conditions, giving pilots the confidence to push their flights further. Experience effortless setup, reliable power, and unmatched stability in every flight.
