# BETAFPV 2S Whoop Cable Pigtail (BT2.0)

## 产品介绍

This BT2.0 double connectors cable is for 2S whoop drone like Beta65X HD and Beta65X FPV. With the BT2.0 connector, it can solve the problem of voltage sag, the discharge of the 1S lipo battery becomes more efficient. Pilots can enjoy more flight time without disturbance of low voltage warning.

### Bullet Point

-
Specialized for 2S whoop drone like Beta65X HD and Beta65X FPV.

- With the light and customized 22AWG wire, it will make the drone more lighter. The wire uses US standard core 0.08mm*60. The diameter of silicone outer layer is only 1.5mm, which is 0.2mm thinner than 22AWG standard wire.

- BT2.0 connector decreased internal resistance of connector effectively by upgrading the crimped pins to 1.0mm banana connector and increasing the cross-sectional area of 1.0mm banana connector.

- BT2.0 connector supports 9A continuous current and 15A burst, while the PH2.0 connector supports 4.5A continuous current. Brings more perfect flight experience when you fly the 1S brushless whoop drone.

- By using the BT2.0 connector, it can solve the problem of voltage sag, the discharge of the 1S lipo battery becomes more efficient. Pilots can enjoy more flight time without disturbance of low voltage warning.
