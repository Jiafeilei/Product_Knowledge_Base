# BETAFPV Meteor65 Pro II Brushless Whoop Frame

## 产品介绍

The Meteor65 Pro II Frame revolutionizes whoop frame design, delivering superior flight performance, extended flight times, and exceptional durability. Featuring an enlarged 70mm wheelbase, it provides a more stable and responsive flight base. Aerodynamic refinements—including reduced arm braces and curved duct design—improve flight efficiency, while a widened battery slot accommodates larger 480mAh batteries for extended sessions. With simplified installation through four shock-absorbing mounting holes and seamless Canopy II compatibility, the frame guarantees stable, jello-free footage and effortless maintenance. Enhanced stability and rock-solid durability make it perfect for daily training, micro aerial photography, education, and competitions.

### Larger Wheelbase & Optimized Aerodynamics

The wheelbase has been innovatively enlarged from 66mm to 70mm, providing a more stable and responsive flight base. The arm braces have been refined from 6 to 5 and the whoop duct has been aerodynamically optimized to minimize air resistance and weight, resulting in improved flight efficiency and overall performance for the Meteor65 Pro II.

### Enhanced Battery Capacity

Thanks to the enlarged wheelbase, the battery slot has been widened to 16.1 * 6.3mm, accommodating larger and more powerful LAVA II 1S 480mAh batteries. This upgrade significantly extends flight times, allowing pilots to enjoy longer sessions without compromise.

### Simple Installation & Unmatched Durability

The entire frame has been strengthened to deliver the same rugged toughness as the Meteor75 Pro frame, ensuring long-lasting reliability for daily use. Four new installation holes for shock-absorbing balls, paried with the Canopy II for O4 Air Unit, simplify both installation and removal while delivering stable, jello-free footage performance. This durable and low-maintenance design makes the Meteor65 Pro II ideal for daily training, micro aerial photography, educational programs, and youth competitions.
