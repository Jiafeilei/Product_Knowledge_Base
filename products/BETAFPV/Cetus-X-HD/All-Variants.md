- Gently press the digital bind button on the side of the quadcopter with a screwdriver for one second, and the LED light on the digital VTX board to flash red

- Power on the Walksnail HD goggles and wait for it to complete initialization.

- Press the digital bind button on the Walksnail HD goggles with a pointed object for one second, following a "beep beep" sound

- If the LED light on the digital VTX board turns solid green and the Walksnail HD goggles receive the camera's video image, it means the binding is successful
