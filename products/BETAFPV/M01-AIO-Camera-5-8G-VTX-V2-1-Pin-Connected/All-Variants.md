# BETAFPV M01 AIO Camera 5.8G VTX V2.1(Pin-Connected)

## 产品介绍

M01 AIO Camera 5.8G VTX V2.1 is upgraded into a pin-connected version, easy to connect with the camera.  With the ultra-lightweight 2.72g. It comes with a super Mini 5.8GHz FPV Transmitter, 1200TVL FPV Camera (C02), and RG178 Antenna features 6 bands and 37 channels on the 5.8GHz band with Raceband which is perfect for Pro Series and Brushed Whoop Drone.

Note: If you are looking for AIO VTX for the Pro2 Series whoop drone, Please click here to reach out.

### Bullet Point

- Upgraded into pin-connected version, easy to assemble

- Lighter than Z02 AIO Camera VTX, perfect for Micro Drone

- Suitable for Pro Series and Brushed Whoop Drone

- 30° tilt Camera Mounting hardware is provided to suit your preference

### Specification

- Item: M01 Camera AIO VTX V2.1

- Camera: C02 FPV Micro Camera

- TVL: 1200TVL

- FOV: H:120°/V:100°

-
Video System: NTSC

- Horizontal Resolution: 640X480 1/4”

- Output power: 25mW

- Supply Voltage: 5V

- Size: 18*14*4.5mm(VTX)

- Weight: 2.72g

- Antenna: RG178 Antenna

- Frequency: 5.8GHz 6 bands 37 channels, with Raceband: 5362~5945MHz
