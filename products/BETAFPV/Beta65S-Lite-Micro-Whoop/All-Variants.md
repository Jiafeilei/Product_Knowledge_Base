# BETAFPV Beta65S Lite Micro Whoop

## 产品介绍

Beta65S Lite is a brushed whoop FPV drone with BETAFPV Lite flight controller. Powered by LiteSilver firmware, much simple and much affordable.  It is ready to fly and the FC firmware does not require much setting as Betaflight does.

### Lite Brushed FC V3

The Lite brushed FC V3 is available with a series of improvements based on V2 version. Comes with the V3 board, the PC configurator is also available. It has the ability to change settings for custom builds or to adapt to personal preferences.

User Manual for Lite Brushed FC V3 board and Beta65S Lite drone.

###
Lite Brushed FC V2

This version is discontinued and will not be available for sales. We recommend you buy its great alternative V3 version.

The stock firmware in the V2 board is upgraded since 2020 December. This new firmware is also used on Starter Kit 2 and Beta65S Lite drones at the same time. Please go to the Lite brushed FC V2 page for the manual.

Beta65S Lite Drone User Manual Download.

###
Bullet Point

- As same as the Beta65S, more punch on the throttle with faster 19000KV 7x16mm motors and 4-blade propellers.

- Support level mode and acro mode. Also, it has the ability to change settings for custom builds or to adapt to personal preferences by stick gestures.

- Built-in protocol native receiver, compatible with almost radio transmitter.

### Specification

- Wheelbase: 65mm

- Weight: 25g (without battery)

- Motor: 7x16mm 19000KV Brushed Motors

- Canopy: Canopy for Micro Camera

- Flight time: 3-4 minutes
